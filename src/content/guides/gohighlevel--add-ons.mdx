## Does a small business need any GoHighLevel add-ons?

Usually not. <T id="gohighlevel" />'s Starter plan, at <Price tool="gohighlevel" plan="starter" /> a month, already includes the CRM, the website and funnel builder, calendars, workflows, reviews, courses and payments, plus 24/7 support. What pushes a single business's bill up is usage, meaning texts, calls, email and AI drawn from a prepaid wallet, which the [full GoHighLevel price breakdown](/tools/gohighlevel/pricing/) covers. Most of HighLevel's add-ons exist for agencies serving clients.

Five are worth a look for a single business:

- **AI Employee**, if you want AI answering chats and calls. The Growth plan costs $50 a month per sub-account and includes 1,000 Conversation AI agent responses and 100 Voice AI minutes a month; Unlimited costs $97, subject to fair use. Calls the AI handles still carry phone charges.
- **WordPress hosting**, if you are keeping a WordPress site rather than rebuilding it in GoHighLevel. Standard costs $10 a month for one site, or $108 a year.
- **Online listings management**, $30 a month per sub-account, or $150 for six months or $300 a year, with a 3-month commitment. HighLevel says it helps keep your address, opening hours and phone number consistent across 70 or more websites.
- **HIPAA compliance or WhatsApp**, each covered below, for medical practices and for businesses whose customers message them on WhatsApp.

The rest can wait. Premium Support, at $500 a month, costs more than five times the Starter plan. A dedicated email IP, $59 a month per IP, cannot be bought on Starter at all. Workflow Pro only matters if your automations lean on premium steps such as webhooks or Google Sheets; standard workflow steps cost nothing. Advanced Account Setup, a done-with-you setup service, is optional and costs $1,000 one time.

## Which GoHighLevel add-ons are charged per sub-account?

For an agency, the unit beside each add-on matters more than its price, because HighLevel bills the agency for every sub-account where a per-sub-account add-on is switched on. This is how each one was billed when we checked on <Checked tool="gohighlevel" />:

- **Per enabled sub-account, each month:** AI Employee at $50 (Growth) or $97 (Unlimited), SEO powered by Search Atlas at $79, the Branded Client Portal App at $49, online listings at $30, the premium prospecting tool at $29, WhatsApp at $10, and Workflow Pro at $10, $25 or $50 depending on the tier.
- **Per IP or per site:** a dedicated email IP at $59 per IP, and WordPress hosting at $10 per site on Standard, $220 a month for 25 sites on Utility, or $497 a month for unlimited sites on Truly Unlimited.
- **A flat monthly fee, not charged per sub-account:** HIPAA compliance at $297 for the whole account, Premium Support at $500, the white label mobile app at $497, the HighLevel Certification Program at $97 and the Whitelabel Zap at $50.

The first group is where agency bills climb. Ten clients on the SEO add-on cost $790 a month, more than the <Price tool="gohighlevel" plan="agency-pro" /> Agency Pro plan that runs the whole agency.

HighLevel always charges the agency first. Under Agency View > Reselling, an agency can deploy a service directly and absorb the cost, or let a client buy it inside their own sub-account at a price the agency sets. HighLevel's Terms let that price exceed HighLevel's own only on the $497 Agency Pro plan, and its pricing guide says rebilling AI Employee needs the same plan. Its rebilling article adds that monthly fees such as the WhatsApp subscription and WordPress hosting go straight to the card on file, not through the usage wallet.

Budget Workflow Pro per client too. HighLevel's pricing guide calls the $10 tier a price "for the agency" in one line but marks Workflow Pro as per enabled sub-account in its table, while the Workflow Pro article counts allowances per sub-account and describes buying a plan from inside a sub-account. The [marketing agency stack](/stacks/agency/) shows the rest of an agency's software bill.

## What do the white label app and other agency add-ons cost?

Most of these serve the agency itself or the package it sells, so a business running only its own brand can skip them; the Branded Client Portal App is the one to consider if you run a community in GoHighLevel.

- **White label mobile app: $497 a month, or $1,491 a quarter.** Every plan already includes HighLevel's own mobile app; this add-on puts your brand on the app your clients use with their HighLevel tools. It costs as much as the Agency Pro plan, on top of whichever plan you pay for.
- **Branded Client Portal App: $49 a month per enabled sub-account.** A separate product for clients that run communities, giving their members mobile access. Clients order it while logged in to your white-labeled version of HighLevel, HighLevel handles fulfillment, and you set the price they see.
- **Whitelabel Zap: $50 a month.** HighLevel's team builds a Zap carrying your agency's branding that feeds leads into your white-labeled account.
- **Premium prospecting tool: $29 a month per enabled sub-account.** Every plan lists a prospecting tool among its core features; the premium version supports reports with weekly refreshes, AI-powered prospecting, website performance audits, SEO heatmaps and lead-generation widgets for assessing potential clients.
- **HighLevel Certification Program: $97 a month or $970 a year.** Training on the platform and a HighLevel exam. The Terms say a certificate is not an endorsement and does not make its holder a HighLevel representative.
- **Premium Support: $500 a month or $5,000 a year.** Depending on the package, HighLevel says it may add faster response paths, dedicated technical account management, a private Slack channel and quarterly business reviews.

The custom-priced Enterprise plan includes Premium Support, the white label mobile app and HIPAA compliance. An agency about to buy two of those three separately should ask HighLevel for an Enterprise quote first.

## What does GoHighLevel's HIPAA add-on cover, and which practices need it?

HighLevel's pricing guide states that GoHighLevel accounts are not HIPAA compliant by default. Its HIPAA compliance add-on costs $297 a month on any plan and is charged once for the whole account; it is enabled for the agency and then configured for each sub-account that needs it. HighLevel describes it as additional compliance controls for protected health information workflows.

It matters to a medical clinic, dental practice or med spa that will store or send patient information through GoHighLevel, such as intake forms, appointment notes or messages about treatment. HighLevel's own pages set out what the purchase does and does not do:

- **It is a condition of HighLevel's Business Associate Agreement.** The Terms make the BAA depend on an active, fully paid HIPAA subscription. If a payment fails, the agreement ends automatically and you alone are responsible for handling patient data compliantly.
- **HighLevel says it covers LC Email.** Its LC Email article says the built-in email is HIPAA compliant while the package is active, citing a signed BAA with Mailgun.
- **It does not make your practice compliant.** The Terms leave compliance with HIPAA to the customer and say HighLevel does not represent that your use of the platform will comply with any law.
- **AI features are off limits for patient data.** The Terms have customers agree not to enter protected health information into HighLevel's AI features, which matters if you hoped an AI Employee would answer patient messages.
- **It cannot be switched off.** Once enabled, it cannot be deactivated or refunded.

For a single practice on Starter, the add-on costs about three times the plan and lifts the fixed bill to $394 a month before any texts or emails. For an agency with several healthcare clients, the account-wide price works in its favor: one fee, however many practices. Because the add-on cannot be switched off, a practice that uses GoHighLevel only for marketing and keeps patient records elsewhere should check with whoever handles its HIPAA obligations before buying. A practice that needs a CRM but not texting or funnels can compare <T id="zoho-crm" />: its Standard plan, at <Price tool="zoho-crm" plan="standard" /> per user a month, lists HIPAA compliance among its features, though Zoho's pricing pages list no built-in texting.

## Which businesses should add WhatsApp to GoHighLevel?

GoHighLevel's WhatsApp integration costs $10 a month for each sub-account with WhatsApp switched on, plus per-message charges that depend on the destination country and the message category. It suits a business whose customers already message it on WhatsApp, or one selling into countries where people prefer WhatsApp to texts, because the add-on brings those conversations into GoHighLevel.

For messages to US and Canadian numbers, the choice turns on the message type. A utility template, the category for order updates and appointment reminders, costs $0.0036 per delivered message, less than half of a one-segment text at $0.00747 plus its carrier fee. A marketing template costs $0.0263, more than twice that text. Per message, reminders cost less on WhatsApp and promotions cost less by text.

Outside the US and Canada the spread is wide: HighLevel's rate table prices marketing messages to Germany and the Netherlands at more than five times the US rate, and to India and Turkey at under half of it. Look up your customers' countries before budgeting.

Since 1 October 2026, utility templates sent inside the 24-hour customer service window are billed, and so are free-form service messages beyond the first 1,000 per WhatsApp phone number each month. Because replies that used to cost nothing now carry a charge, HighLevel's guide tells agencies to review their rebilling multiplier, which defaults to 1.05x to cover payment processing, and any fixed-price client agreements.

## Which GoHighLevel add-ons are hard to cancel?

Three commitments sit outside the headline prices:

- **HIPAA compliance** cannot be deactivated once enabled, and HighLevel's Terms name the HIPAA package among add-ons that cannot be canceled or refunded after HighLevel switches them on.
- **Online listings** carries a 3-month commitment, and canceling it means giving HighLevel support the location name, location ID and a reason through chat.
- **A plan downgrade leaves add-ons running.** HighLevel's billing article says downgrading does not automatically cancel add-ons, Marketplace apps or phone numbers, so each one needs removing on its own.

The Pause Plan, $27 a month, is HighLevel's suggested way to keep an account's data instead of canceling, since a canceled account's data is deleted after 90 days. The same billing article also says agency accounts cannot be paused, so confirm with HighLevel's billing team that yours qualifies before counting on it. And if an invoice shows a second charge of $97 or $297, HighLevel's billing FAQ notes that an AI Employee plan or the HIPAA add-on can share a plan's price, so it may not be a duplicate.

The safe order is the same for every buyer: start with the plan alone, add an add-on only for a named client or a named need, and check whether it is billed per sub-account before switching it on.
