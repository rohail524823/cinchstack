## Which HubSpot plan actually fits a small team right now?

For a single operator with a small list, Marketing Hub Starter is the plan to buy, not the free tools. The free tier caps out at 1,000 contacts and 2,000 marketing emails a month, puts HubSpot branding on every send, and allows only one automated email action — enough to test the product, not to run a list. Starter removes that branding, raises the ceiling to 10 automated actions, and costs <Price tool="hubspot" plan="starter" /> a seat a month. A one-person business with 1,000 contacts and normal email volume needs exactly one seat, so the real bill is <Real tool="hubspot" size="solo" /> a month. The one thing Starter can't do at any team size is send marketing text messages: HubSpot sells that add-on only with Professional or Enterprise, so a solo operator who specifically needs SMS ends up paying for a much bigger plan to unlock a feature a handful of texts a month wouldn't otherwise justify.

A team of three still fits Starter if it can live without SMS. Extra seats cost a flat $20 a month each, and contacts above the 1,000 included are billed in bands starting at $50 per 1,000 a month. At this size, the case for moving up almost never comes down to seats or contacts — it comes down to one specific thing Starter doesn't sell.

## What changes the moment you move from Starter to Professional?

Professional isn't Starter with extra features bolted on; it's a different shape of bill. Starter charges per seat, with no base fee and no minimum term. Professional charges a flat monthly base that already includes 3 seats, adds a required 12-month commitment, and comes with a one-time onboarding fee before you send a single email. Paid monthly on that commitment, the base runs <Price tool="hubspot" plan="professional" /> a month; prepay for the year and it drops to <Price tool="hubspot" plan="professional" field="annualMonthly" /> a month — there's no cancel-anytime monthly option at this tier the way there is on Starter.

Seats and contacts move differently, too. A fourth seat on Professional costs $50 a month paid monthly, or $45 billed annually, well above Starter's flat $20. Included marketing contacts drop from 1,000 for the whole account on Starter to 2,000 on Professional, and more are sold in 5,000-contact packs starting at $250 a month rather than Starter's 1,000-contact bands. Then there's the fee due before any of that: a $3,000 Professional Onboarding fee, charged automatically and required, not optional, the first time you buy the plan. Run the numbers for a 3-seat team with 5,000 contacts and marketing SMS switched on, and HubSpot's own calculator puts the ongoing bill at <Real tool="hubspot" size="small" /> a month billed annually — before that onboarding fee lands on the first invoice. Enterprise goes further again: a higher, annual-only base plus a $7,000 onboarding fee, a jump most small teams never need to make.

## Is the discounted Starter seat price the one you'll actually keep paying?

HubSpot currently discounts Starter for new customers: its calculator prices a seat at $10 a month billed monthly, against the $20 list price shown struck through beside it. That's a real number if you're comparing plans today, but treat it as temporary. HubSpot's own terms allow fees to rise to the catalog list price at renewal, with 30 days' notice, and the offer page says only that it's for new customers and available for a limited time — not how long it lasts or what your specific renewal will look like. Budget for $20 a seat, the list price, if you're planning a year or more out, not the discounted number on today's screen.

This matters less once you're on Professional, because there's no discounted on-ramp there. The $890 base, or $800 prepaid, the extra-seat prices, and the $3,000 onboarding fee are all list price; HubSpot's public pricing page shows no new-customer offer at that tier. Starter is the one plan on this page where the sticker price and the price you're quoted might not match for long.

## What pushes a HubSpot bill higher as contacts and texts grow?

Two billing rules matter more than any single price. First, marketing contact tiers only move up during your term: cross into the next tier at any point and HubSpot bills it immediately, and marking contacts as non-marketing to bring the count back down only takes effect at the start of the next calendar month or at renewal, not right away. Second, every Core Seat across every hub you own is priced at your highest edition — pair Marketing Hub Professional with a higher-tier Sales or Service Hub, and every marketing seat gets priced up too, not just the ones doing sales work.

Marketing SMS adds its own layer of cost and geography. The add-on costs $75 a month for 1,000 message segments and only works for U.S. numbers texting U.S. and Canadian recipients; going over the allowance costs $15 a month for each extra 1,000-segment pack. Scale that up: an 8-person team on Professional with 25,000 contacts and 5,000 texts a month — seats, contact packs, the SMS add-on and four extra segment packs included — comes to <Real tool="hubspot" size="growing" /> a month billed annually, and that's still before the $3,000 onboarding fee on the first bill.
