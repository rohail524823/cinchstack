## Which systeme.io plan fits a course creator or freelancer?

Systeme.io tiers its plans mostly by contact count, and that is the right first filter. Under 2,000 contacts, stay on Free: it needs no credit card, sends unlimited emails, and includes one course with up to 500 students. Once contacts pass 2,000, or sales get close to the free-plan cap covered below, <Price tool="systeme-io" plan="startup" /> a month buys Startup — the plan behind the solo bill above.

Startup's own limits show up fast: 10 tags, 10 automation rules and 10 sales funnels, with no evergreen webinars at all. A freelancer running more than a couple of active campaigns, or a course creator who wants an evergreen webinar funnel, needs Webinar at <Price tool="systeme-io" plan="webinar" /> a month instead — the plan behind the small-team bill above, with room for 10,000 contacts, 100 automation rules and 10 evergreen webinars. Unlimited, at <Price tool="systeme-io" plan="unlimited" /> a month, raises the CRM pipeline count to 100 and is the plan behind the growing-team bill above; it suits a busy course business running several staff accounts. Its name oversells it slightly, though: the pricing page markets Unlimited's contacts and product variants as unlimited, but the plans API behind it caps them at 1,000,000 contacts and 5,000 variants — high enough that almost no small business will hit it, but not literally unlimited. Above Unlimited sits a fifth tier, For Teams, which adds a dedicated success manager at a price given only on a sales call.

## What's the catch with the systeme.io free plan?

Systeme.io's terms, not its pricing page, cap each Free account at $10,000 in lifetime sales and then allow 3 days to upgrade before its payment pages stop taking new sales. That cap, the single automation rule, the badge you cannot remove and when each one forces an upgrade are covered in our guide to the [Systeme.io free plan limits](/tools/systeme-io/free-plan/).

## What makes a systeme.io bill go up during the month?

Three things move the number beyond the plan price. First, running over your limits: go past your plan's contact, funnel or course cap and systeme.io emails a 3-day warning, then automatically upgrades the subscription and bills the next plan's price for the rest of that billing period — there's no smaller top-up, only the next full plan. Second, card processing: systeme.io itself takes 0% of every sale, but whichever processor you connect still charges its own rate; Stripe's standard US rate is 2.9% plus 30¢ per domestic charge, on top of the systeme.io subscription. Third, SMS: systeme.io has no messaging plan of its own, so turning it on means connecting your own Twilio account and paying Twilio's rates directly, not systeme.io's — its help center is explicit that SMS costs and delivery are managed through Twilio.

Sellers with a large physical-product catalog hit a different wall entirely. Product variants are capped at 50 on Free, 100 on Startup, 250 on Webinar and, per the plans API, 5,000 even on Unlimited (see above); <T id="woocommerce" /> has no such cap on its free core plugin.

## Is billing systeme.io annually worth it?

Paying yearly drops every paid plan from 12 payments to 10, billed as one charge for the year: Startup falls to <Price tool="systeme-io" plan="startup" field="annualMonthly" /> a month, Webinar to <Price tool="systeme-io" plan="webinar" field="annualMonthly" /> and Unlimited to <Price tool="systeme-io" plan="unlimited" field="annualMonthly" />. Systeme.io's own pricing page calls this "2 months free." Annual billing on any plan, or the Unlimited plan even month-to-month, also comes with a free done-for-you migration from your old platform — a perk monthly Startup and Webinar customers don't get.

The commitment cuts both ways. Subscriptions are non-refundable under systeme.io's terms, whatever the billing period, so a yearly plan locks in a full year's spend with no money-back option if the business changes course. Downgrading isn't self-serve either way: you contact support, they send a custom order form, and you have to fit the lower plan's limits before they'll process it.

## Where does the systeme.io pricing page contradict itself?

The pricing page's own FAQ disagrees with its comparison table in three places. The FAQ, marked "last updated: April 2026," claims Startup has unlimited funnels and Webinar has unlimited everything, but the comparison table and the plans API both cap Startup at 10 funnels and Webinar at 50. The same FAQ's "all plans include" section promises unlimited students, while the table and API cap Free at 500. And the FAQ says you can downgrade from your account settings, while the help center describes a process that goes through support instead. This page follows the comparison table, the plans API and the help center wherever they disagree with the FAQ.

One more rule is worth knowing before you pay by card: upgrading turns the unused time on your current plan into a free trial of the new one before the full new price is charged. PayPal can't prorate that way, so a PayPal subscriber who upgrades pays the full new price immediately and has to ask support to cancel the old subscription separately.
