ClickFunnels prices its four tiers by workspace count, team-member seats and contacts, and its own FAQ is blunt about the one thing it doesn't charge for: a cut of sales. What the page is less clear about is which of those three numbers actually forces an upgrade, whether its advertised annual savings add up, and how long its free trial runs.

## Which ClickFunnels plan fits a solo funnel builder versus an agency?

ClickFunnels prices Launch, Scale and Optimize by three limits at once, and in practice the team-seat cap decides the plan long before contacts do. A solo funnel builder selling around $3,000 a month, with only the owner logged in, fits Launch at <Price tool="clickfunnels" plan="launch" /> a month comfortably: its 2 included seats cover one person, and its 10,000-contact ceiling is nowhere near a business this size. Launch is the plan behind the solo bill above.

A small team running 3 staff accounts on $30,000 a month in sales needs more seats than Launch allows well before its contact list gets anywhere close to Launch's cap. That seat limit is what forces Scale at <Price tool="clickfunnels" plan="scale" /> a month — the plan behind the small-team bill above, with room for 5 team members and 75,000 contacts. The same pattern repeats: a growing team with 8 staff accounts outgrows Scale's 5-seat cap well before its 75,000-contact ceiling, so it needs Optimize at <Price tool="clickfunnels" plan="optimize" /> a month, the plan behind the growing-team bill above, which allows 10 team members and 150,000 contacts.

What makes ClickFunnels a plausible pick for an agency specifically is its workspace count: Launch includes 1, Scale 5, Optimize 10 and Dominate 20, so one subscription can run several client sub-accounts rather than paying separately per client.

## What forces a ClickFunnels bill up before you hit a contact limit?

Team-member seats are ClickFunnels' tightest constraint, and there is no published add-on to buy extra seats without upgrading the whole plan. Launch caps out at 2 team members, Scale at 5, Optimize at 10 and Dominate at 20; cross that line and the only documented route up is the next full tier, whatever else it costs.

Email sends follow the same shape, with no add-on either: 50,000 a month on Launch, 300,000 on Scale, 750,000 on Optimize and 1,200,000 on Dominate. AI tools carry their own word allowance too — 1M words on Launch up to 10M on Dominate — though ClickFunnels' pricing page never says whether that allowance resets every month or is a one-time credit.

Every plan also markets "Unlimited Funnels," but flags it "Limited Time" on the pricing page itself — ClickFunnels' own hint that a numeric funnel cap could come back.

## Does ClickFunnels' annual discount add up to what it claims?

Run the arithmetic on Launch and the label is off: 12 times the gap between its $97 monthly price and its $81 annual-equivalent price comes to $192, not the $194 the page claims to save. The same math falls a few dollars short on Scale and Optimize too, suggesting the displayed annual price is rounded from a total the page never actually shows.

Dominate skips the choice entirely. It disappears from the plan grid the moment you click the "Monthly Plans" toggle, and its only listed price is $5,997 a year, about <Price tool="clickfunnels" plan="dominate" field="annualMonthly" /> a month, with no "Save" label at all. One more default-view detail: clicking Launch's "Start Free Trial" button from the default annual view opens a signup address built for annual billing, so a shopper who never touched the monthly toggle may start a trial that defaults to yearly.

Every ClickFunnels plan carries the same 30-day money-back guarantee regardless of billing period, and ClickFunnels' pricing-page FAQ says plans can be changed at any time.

## What does ClickFunnels not say about its free trial?

ClickFunnels puts a "Start Free Trial" button on Launch, Scale and Optimize, but clicking it only opens a two-step signup that asks for a name and email on step one. Reaching step two, where billing terms would likely appear, requires real signup data, so as of <Checked tool="clickfunnels" />, neither the trial's length nor whether a card is required could be confirmed from ClickFunnels' current site. Third-party sites and ClickFunnels' own support articles for the older "Classic" product describe a 14-day trial that charges the card on file automatically when it ends, but that content is written for the legacy platform, not the one priced on this page, so it is not a reliable stand-in here.

Two more gaps sit alongside it. ClickFunnels' page never says what happens when a workspace goes over its contact, email-send or workspace limit: no automatic upgrade, overage bill or lock is documented. And its FAQ answers on custom domains and hosting were never expanded, so domain availability is inferred only from each plan's own domain-count line.

## Is ClickFunnels really free of transaction fees?

On the platform fee, yes. ClickFunnels' own pricing-page FAQ asks, "Does ClickFunnels take a percentage of my sales?" and answers, "No. You'll keep every penny you make." That holds on every plan, Launch through Dominate. Card processing is a separate cost every seller pays somewhere, and ClickFunnels' support documentation puts its rate at 2.9% plus 30 cents per transaction, the same on both gateways it supports, Stripe and its own Payments AI. Optional extras can stack on top of either one — Stripe's separate Billing, Invoicing and Tax add-ons, or Payments AI's bundled version of the same three — but the base card rate itself carries no ClickFunnels markup.
