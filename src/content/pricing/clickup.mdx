ClickUp's pricing page leads with per-seat plan prices, but two of its biggest real costs sit outside that table: a set of feature "uses" that quietly cap out and never come back, and AI add-ons billed per member on top of whatever plan a Workspace already pays for. Here is which plan actually fits each size of team, and where the real bill diverges from the sticker price.

## Which ClickUp plan fits a solo user, a small team or a growing one?

ClickUp bills every paid plan per Workspace member, so the real question for each size of business is which plan its people need, not just which plan costs least on paper. A solo operator could run ClickUp on Free Forever, at <Price tool="clickup" plan="free-forever" /> a month, but its 60MB total storage cap and its one-time allowance of Custom Field and Gantt "uses" rule it out for anyone who plans to stick around past a first project, so the real solo bill above, <Real tool="clickup" size="solo" /> a month, runs on Unlimited instead.

A small team of five members fits the same Unlimited plan, for <Real tool="clickup" size="small" /> a month, and picks up unlimited storage, Custom Fields and Gantt charts in the process. What Unlimited does not lift is the Dashboards, Timeline and Workload cap: still 100 uses each per Workspace, never reset, whether one person hits that ceiling or fifty share it. A growing team of 15 members typically runs into that ceiling hard enough to need Business, which is why the real growing-team bill above, <Real tool="clickup" size="growing" /> a month, runs on Business rather than Unlimited — Business lifts the 100-use cap entirely and raises the monthly Automation-action allowance from Unlimited's 1,000 to 5,000.

## What are ClickUp's "uses," and why do they never reset?

ClickUp's plan-comparison table lists Custom Fields, Gantt charts, Timeline, Workload and Dashboards as included on every plan, but "included" does not mean unlimited until Unlimited or Business actually lifts each one. Free Forever grants a one-time allowance of 60 uses each for Custom Fields, Gantt, Timeline, Workload and Dashboards, plus 100 time-tracking uses, and none of it refills once spent. Unlimited removes the Custom Field and Gantt caps for good but keeps its own version for Dashboards, Timeline and Workload, at a higher but still fixed 100 uses per Workspace — shared by everyone in it, not issued per person.

ClickUp's own pricing FAQ defines a "use" loosely enough to catch a team off guard: opening a Dashboard counts the same as building one. A team that checks a shared Dashboard daily can burn through Unlimited's 100-use allowance in weeks, long before anyone intended to create 100 different Dashboards. ClickUp says it grants a grace period once a Workspace crosses a limit, but the durable fix is the same either way: move to Business, which drops the 100-use ceiling on Dashboards, Timeline and Workload entirely.

## How much do Brain AI and Everything AI really add to the bill?

ClickUp never bundles AI into a plan price; Brain AI and Everything AI are separate add-ons layered on top of whatever plan a Workspace already pays for. Brain AI costs $14 per Workspace member a month billed monthly, or $9 billed yearly, for unlimited fair-use AI chat and writing plus a monthly pool of AI Super Credits. Everything AI costs $33 a month or $28 yearly and adds AI Notetaker, Ambient Answers and Autopilot Agents on top of everything Brain AI includes.

The part easy to miss: both add-ons bill for every member in the Workspace, not only the people who actually touch AI, and that includes a seat a removed member left behind until someone new fills it or the add-on is dropped entirely. Because Brain AI's own per-member price already runs higher than Unlimited's seat price, and Everything AI's higher still than Business's, turning either add-on on for a whole team can add more to the bill than the underlying plan itself costs. Free Forever Workspaces can't buy either add-on at all: the only AI there is a one-time trial of 25 Brain AI uses for a Workspace of up to 10 people.

## Is ClickUp's yearly billing worth it, and how hard is it to reverse?

Yearly billing is cheaper on every plan and add-on ClickUp sells, but its own "up to" framing is not a reliable ceiling. Unlimited drops from <Price tool="clickup" plan="unlimited" /> to <Price tool="clickup" plan="unlimited" field="annualMonthly" /> a member, exactly matching the "Save up to 30% with yearly" ClickUp advertises for plans, while Business drops from <Price tool="clickup" plan="business" /> to <Price tool="clickup" plan="business" field="annualMonthly" /> — a real saving of about 37%, beyond what that same 30% headline promises. The AI add-ons run the opposite way at least once: Brain AI's yearly rate saves close to 36%, again past ClickUp's separate "up to 20%" claim for AI, while Everything AI's yearly rate saves only around 15%, short of it. None of the four numbers is wrong, but only one of the four actually matches ClickUp's own advertised percentage.

The bigger catch is switching, not the math. ClickUp won't let one Workspace run monthly and yearly licenses side by side, and moving from monthly to yearly isn't a self-serve toggle: it takes contacting Support, and the switch bills the next 12 months immediately at the new rate. A Workspace unsure of its headcount for the year ahead should stay on monthly rather than lock in a rate it might outgrow, or shrink past, before the term is up.

## What else can push a ClickUp Workspace's bill above the plan price?

A few mechanics in the add-ons and fine print above are easy to miss until they hit. Run out of permission-controlled guest seats — 5 plus 2 per member on Unlimited, 10 plus 5 per member on Business — and inviting one more guest doesn't fail; ClickUp adds a new paid member seat automatically instead, which raises the guest allowance and then stays on the bill. AI Super Credits meter separately from either AI add-on, at $10 per 10,000 credits a month, and once a Workspace uses just over its allowance, Super Agents, AI Fields and image generation stop working until credits renew the next month unless it buys more.

The money-back guarantee ClickUp advertises is narrower than the pricing page implies, too: its help center limits the full refund to a new customer's first paid-plan subscription, not to every renewal or every add-on bought afterward. And one real question ClickUp's own help center never answers: whether a seat a removed member leaves behind keeps costing money at the next renewal if nobody fills it, or drops off the bill — the documentation only says the seat "remains open."

## Is ClickUp or Notion the better fit for a small team's docs and projects?

<T id="notion" /> competes for the same job as ClickUp: a per-member workspace for docs and projects, priced across comparable free, mid-tier and top-tier plans. ClickUp's own plan pages list Gantt charts, Timeline, Workload views, Dashboards and Automations starting on Free Forever, none of which appear on Notion's plan cards; Notion answers back by folding its AI assistant, Enterprise Search and meeting notes into a single Business seat price, where ClickUp sells comparable AI only as a separate per-member add-on stacked on top of any plan.

On seat price alone, ClickUp's Unlimited plan costs less than Notion's Plus at small-team size, and that ordering reverses at growing-team size once ClickUp's Dashboards, Timeline and Workload caps force the move to Business. A team organized around dashboards, Gantt charts and workload views across several projects fits ClickUp's model more directly; a team that mainly writes and organizes shared documents fits Notion's.
