## Who should stay on Semrush instead of switching?

<T id="semrush" /> earns its price for a specific kind of business: daily rank tracking, site audits and AI-search visibility bundled under one login, aimed at an agency running several client sites, a freelancer with an active SEO practice, or a SaaS startup that wants classic rankings and AI-answer mentions from the same dashboard. If your team already sits on the SEO plan at <Price tool="semrush" plan="seo" /> a month tracking close to 500 keywords, or on Pro+ tracking 1,500, switching mainly to save a little money on a like-for-like plan rarely covers the work of rebuilding projects elsewhere. The strongest reason to stay: Semrush updates rankings daily on every paid plan, and Ahrefs' Rank Tracker only manages weekly updates at any price it sells. Anyone who just wants an occasional check, not ongoing tracking, should compare Semrush's own free plan before shopping for a competitor at all — 10 reports a day and 10 tracked keywords, no card required.

## What actually pushes people to look for a Semrush alternative?

Six patterns cover almost every real search for an alternative. Keyword tracking has a hard ceiling: SEO and Starter both stop at 500 tracked keywords, and the only documented way past that is the full jump to Pro+ at <Price tool="semrush" plan="pro-plus" /> a month, with nothing smaller sold in between. Extra logins get pricier as the plan does: a second person costs $45 a month on SEO or Starter, $80 on Pro+, or $100 on Advanced, and every one of them gets SEO access only — giving that person AI Visibility too costs another $99 on top. API access exists on exactly one plan, Advanced, at <Price tool="semrush" plan="advanced" /> a month, a lot to pay just to pull your own ranking data into a script. Testing a paid plan means handing over a card for a 7-day trial that opens with a refundable authorization charge, and the pricing page shows no trial button on Advanced at all. The free plan's 10 daily reports run out fast for anyone doing more than the occasional lookup, and the next step up is the full SEO plan, with nothing smaller in between. And Site Audit's crawl allowance on SEO, 100,000 pages a month split across 5 monitored sites, 20,000 each, is thin for a business auditing several larger sites at once.

## What should you check before you cancel Semrush?

Billing works differently depending on how you pay. A month-to-month subscription is not refundable at all, and Semrush's cancellation and refund policy limits its 7-day money-back guarantee to an initial annual subscription or an initial add-on purchase made by card; cancel later and the plan simply runs to the end of the term you already paid for. Paying annually means one charge for the full year up front, not 12 separate monthly payments, so a toolkit or add-on attached mid-term gets billed once, prorated to the end of that annual term rather than spread out. If your account also carries the AI Visibility Toolkit, or any of the separate Local, Content, Social, Advertising or AI PR toolkits, check each one's own billing cycle before canceling the core plan, since they are sold and billed as their own subscriptions, not folded into the SEO plan's term. Time any comparison shopping for before your current term renews: the 7-day money-back window only ever applies to a fresh annual purchase, and a month-to-month plan refunds nothing once you've paid for it.

## How do you move your SEO data off Semrush?

Start the move before the account lapses, not after. Semrush's plan pages don't advertise a one-click export of Position Tracking history, Site Audit findings or keyword lists into another platform's format, and neither Ahrefs nor SE Ranking publishes a tool built to read a Semrush export directly, so expect to rebuild tracked keyword lists and audit settings by hand on whichever tool you land on. SE Ranking is worth knowing about here specifically: paying for an annual subscription there includes free assisted migration from another platform, while a monthly subscriber only gets self-serve import tools. Whatever you move to, keep the Semrush login active long enough to pull the historical reports you will want for a before-and-after comparison, since that history does not travel with you automatically.
