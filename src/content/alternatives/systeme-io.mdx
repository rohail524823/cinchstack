## Who should stick with Systeme.io instead of switching?

Systeme.io fits best for a course creator or freelancer selling through a handful of funnels who wants checkout, email and course delivery under one login. The Free plan already includes a funnel builder, one course for up to 500 students, unlimited email sends, and a 0% cut of every sale, with no card required to start. <Price tool="systeme-io" plan="startup" /> a month buys Startup once contacts pass 2,000, and every plan, Free included, allows unlimited staff accounts and unlimited sub-accounts at no extra charge. That combination, a funnel, an inbox and a course host that takes nothing from the sale itself, is hard to match at this price anywhere else on this page. A business selling one flagship course, a coaching program or a short list of digital offers, with no physical inventory and no need for native texting, usually gets more from staying and learning Systeme.io's limits than from rebuilding the same funnels elsewhere for little real gain. If that sounds like your business, the more useful next step is the catches below, not a replacement.

## What actually pushes people to look for a Systeme.io alternative?

Three limits account for most searches that land here, and none of them is really the price. Texting is not built in: turning on SMS means opening and separately funding your own Twilio account, since Systeme.io publishes no messaging rate of its own and says as much directly in its help centre. Physical-product variants are capped everywhere, climbing from 50 on Free to only 250 on Webinar, and even <Price tool="systeme-io" plan="unlimited" /> Unlimited's own plans API stops at 5,000 despite the pricing page calling that limit unlimited, so a real catalog outgrows the platform fast. And no plan carries a refund window: subscriptions are non-refundable under Systeme.io's own terms, so a wrong tier costs you until the current billing period ends. A fourth, quieter limit shows up once a business is running real campaigns: Startup's 10 tags, 10 automation rules and 10 sales funnels are tight the moment a list or its sequences grow past a beginner setup, and a business built around several client accounts finds that each sub-account only inherits its parent plan's own caps, not a separate allowance. ## What should you check before you cancel or downgrade?

Free costs nothing and needs no card, so walking away from it has no financial cleanup to do. A paying account has three things worth checking first. Subscriptions are non-refundable, so time any cancellation for the end of the billing period you already paid for rather than expecting a partial refund mid-cycle. Downgrading is not self-serve either: you contact support, who send back a custom order form built around the lower plan's limits, and cancellation itself only takes effect just before the next renewal date, not the moment you ask. And if a Free account is close to real revenue, watch its ceiling: once lifetime sales cross USD 10,000, a 3-day window opens before payment pages stop accepting new sales, so line up the upgrade, or the switch, well before that deadline arrives rather than after customers are already turned away.

## How do you move your contacts, funnels and courses off Systeme.io?

Contacts are the one piece Systeme.io actually helps you take with you. Its help centre confirms you select your list from the Contacts screen, choose Export, and a CSV file is generated and emailed to your account address, downloadable for only 3 days before the link expires. Funnels, pages and course content have no matching export tool documented anywhere in Systeme.io's own help centre, so budget real time to rebuild pages, sequences and lessons by hand on whatever replaces it, not a file you import in minutes. The free, done-for-you migration Systeme.io advertises with its Unlimited plan or any annual billing cycle only moves work into the platform; it does nothing for a business moving the other way. Run the new tool alongside Systeme.io for a full billing cycle, sending real traffic to both, and cancel only once the replacement is actually taking payments and delivering course content on its own.
