This notice explains what information CinchStack handles and why. In short: we collect very little.

## Reading the site

Our hosting provider, Netlify, keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) for security and to keep the site running. We also read the aggregated search reports in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, which show how often our pages appear in search results and get clicked, without identifying anyone. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not sell or share personal information.

## Contacting us

If you use the contact form, we receive what you type: your message, the page you mention, and your email address if you give one. The form is processed by Netlify Forms. We use it only to read and reply to your message, and we delete messages when they are no longer needed.

## Project enquiries

If you use the form on the [setup services page](/services/), we receive what you type there: your name and email address, your kind of business and team size, the package you ask about, the tools you use now, what you want built, and, if you give them, your timeline, budget, payment preference, whether you already hire on Upwork, how you found us and the page you came from. The form is processed by Netlify Forms and arrives by email.

We use it to reply, to send a quote and, if you hire Rohail, to set up the work. We keep a dated record of the enquiry, including how you found us, because the freelance marketplace Upwork asks for that record when a client and a freelancer first meet outside it. If you go ahead, invoicing runs through Payoneer, a bank transfer or an Upwork contract, and each of those services handles your payment details under its own privacy terms; we never see card numbers. We delete enquiries that do not lead to work after 12 months, and keep records of paid work as long as tax rules require.

## Affiliate links

When you click an amber affiliate link, you go to the vendor's website, and the vendor or its affiliate network may set a cookie to record that you came from CinchStack. That is how a commission is credited. Those cookies are controlled by the vendor or network under their own privacy policies, not by us. Plain links carry no tracking codes from us.

## Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, using the [contact form](/contact/). We will respond within 30 days.

## Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the bottom of this page changes too.
