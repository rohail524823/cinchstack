CinchStack is free to read and carries no advertising. It earns in two ways: from the editor's [own setup services](#our-own-services), described below, and from **affiliate commissions**: when you sign up for some tools through a link on this site, the vendor pays us a share of what you pay them. You pay the same price either way.

## How to tell which links pay us

- **Amber buttons and amber text links** are affiliate links, and each carries a "Paid link" note beside it. If you sign up through one, the vendor may pay us.
- **Plain buttons and ordinary text links** do not pay us anything.
- Any page with an amber button says so near the top, before the first link.

Affiliate links carry the `rel="sponsored"` attribute, as search engines ask.

## Rules we hold ourselves to

1. **Prices and scores follow the method, not the commission.** Prices come from the vendor's own page. When a tool pays us, or runs a program we have joined or may join, we publish the lower of our two separate scores, as described in [our method](/methodology/#scoring).
2. **The highest-scored option comes first.** In the "If you want one answer" box on each alternatives page, the highest-scored option is shown first, and a tool that pays us appears beside it, not ahead of it.
3. **We recommend tools that do not pay us** whenever they are the better fit, and we say when you need no paid tool at all.
4. **No earnings claims and no invented discounts.** We make no promise that a tool will bring you income or customers, and we mention an offer only when the vendor publishes it.

## Our own services

Besides affiliate commissions, CinchStack's editor, Rohail Nisar, sells [setup and automation work](/services/) to small businesses: stack reviews, CRM setup and migration, automations and AI assistants. Clients pay Rohail directly or through Upwork. Because this is a second way the site's owner earns money from readers, it follows the same kind of rules:

1. **Setup costs the same for every tool in a category we compare**, whether or not the vendor pays us.
2. **Service offers appear only in a labeled box after a page's verdict**, never inside a price table, a score or a pick.
3. **A Stack Check says so when nothing needs buying**: when a free plan, the tool you already have, or the vendor's own onboarding will do the job.
4. **The proof on the services page is Rohail's public Upwork record**, linked so you can check it. We do not publish testimonials we cannot show you the source of.

## Wording some programs require

Some affiliate programs require us to publish specific wording. It appears here and on every page that links to that vendor with an affiliate link.
