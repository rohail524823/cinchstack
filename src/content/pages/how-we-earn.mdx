CinchStack is free to read and carries no advertising. It is funded by **affiliate commissions**: when you sign up for some tools through a link on this site, the vendor pays us a share of what you pay them. You pay the same price either way.

## How to tell which links pay us

- **Amber buttons** are affiliate links. If you sign up through one, the vendor may pay us.
- **Plain buttons and ordinary text links** do not pay us anything.
- Any page with an amber button says so near the top, before the first link.

Affiliate links carry the `rel="sponsored"` attribute, as search engines ask.

## Rules we hold ourselves to

1. **Commission never sets a price, a score or a pick.** Prices come from the vendor's own page. When a tool pays us, or runs a program we have joined or may join, we publish the lower of our two independent scores, as described in [our method](/methodology/#scoring).
2. **A paid pick is never shown alone.** Wherever we recommend a tool that pays us, the highest-scored alternative is shown beside it.
3. **We recommend tools that do not pay us** whenever they are the better fit, and we say when you need no paid tool at all.
4. **No earnings claims and no invented discounts.** We never promise that a tool will bring you income or customers, and we only mention an offer when the vendor publishes it.

## Wording some programs require

Some affiliate programs require us to publish specific wording. It appears here and on every page that links to that vendor with an affiliate link.
