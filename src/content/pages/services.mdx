## Who this is for, and who it is not for

It suits a business of one to twenty people using the kinds of tools this site compares: a CRM, email marketing, a funnel or course platform, a scheduling tool and the automations that join them up.

It is not the right fit for an enterprise rollout across many departments, a voice agent that handles regulated health records, or building a software product from scratch.

## Questions before you send the form

### Do I need this, or can I set it up myself?

Many people can. The pricing and setup notes on each tool page on this site are written so you can. Paid help makes sense when the setup touches several tools at once, when contacts need moving and cleaning, or when your time is worth more spent on the business.

### Will you push me toward a particular tool?

No. The price is the same whichever tool you pick, and the Stack Check says so when the tool you already pay for, a free plan, or the vendor's own onboarding will do the job.

### Do you need my passwords?

No. You invite Rohail to each tool as a user, and remove that access after handover.

### What do the tools themselves cost?

The software is billed to you by each vendor, separately from this work. Each tool's pricing page on this site shows the real monthly cost for a small team.

### What if something breaks later?

Fixes are included for 30 days after handover. After that there is the optional monthly plan in the packages above, or an hourly rate for one-off changes.

### What time zone do you work in?

Pakistan (UTC+5). Calls are booked in your morning or evening, and most of the build happens outside your working hours.

### What if it does not work out?

If the agreed scope cannot be delivered, you get back what you paid for the parts not delivered, and the Stack Check is refunded in full if the report is not delivered on time. Work paid through Upwork follows Upwork's own escrow and dispute terms.
