FAQ
What is Premium plan?

Creating your own website is free. With a Premium plan you can connect your own domain, remove Wix branding from your site, and more. Most Premium plans include a 1 year free domain voucher, which is only valid with a purchase of a new domain from Wix and cannot be used to renew or extend a domain you already own. For best value, we recommend our yearly or multi-year plans.
