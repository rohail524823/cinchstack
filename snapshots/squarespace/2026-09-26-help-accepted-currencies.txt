Accepted currencies for your Squarespace subscriptions
Review a list of currencies you can use to pay for your Squarespace subscriptions.

Last updated October 8, 2025

English

You can pay for your subscription using one of our accepted currencies in the table below. Before choosing a currency, keep in mind:

You'll use the same currency for all payments connected to the website.
It's not possible to change your site's currency after your first payment, or if you move a domain to a trial site with no previous subscriptions. 
Your location at the time of your first payment determines which currency you can choose. For example, if you’re located in Ireland when you make your first payment to Squarespace, your default currency is EUR, but you can also choose from GBP and USD.

Note

If you sign up for Acuity on the Squarespace site or the Acuity site, you can pay for Acuity using one of our accepted currencies above. However, if you have a legacy Acuity plan, you can only pay using in USD. To learn more, visit Acuity Scheduling billing.

Before making your first payment, choose your currency at checkout by selecting the dropdown menu next to Select a plan.

Exchange rates

Subscription prices don’t change based on exchange rates. However, the amount reflected in your bank account may vary based on exchange rates. Contact your bank to learn more about this exchange rate and other possible exchange fees.
