Website refund policy
Information about refunds for your website subscriptions.

Last updated May 21, 2026

English

You can cancel your website subscription at any time. To request a refund, contact us by completing a refund request form.

Refunds for Standard plans

You can request a full refund for annual website subscriptions canceled within 14 days of purchase.

We don’t offer refunds for:

Annual plans canceled after 14 days following the first payment
Monthly plans
Renewal payments - Cancel before the renewal date to avoid being charged. You can find the renewal date on your invoices.
Lost free domain offers - If you cancel an annual plan within 14 days, the cost of the domain is deducted from your refund.
Refunds for Mobile Start plans

Mobile Start plans are purchased through the Squarespace app, which is managed by Apple. To request a refund for this plan, contact Apple.
