Transaction fees and payment processing rates
An overview of the cost associated with selling on Squarespace.

Last updated August 3, 2026

English

If you sell products, collect donations, or charge visitors for access to gated content, review this guide to understand which rates and fees apply to you, how you’re charged, and how to view every rate and fee applied to each of your transactions.

There are two types of costs for completing a sale through Squarespace: payment processing rates and Squarespace transaction fees. Payment processing rates are charged for every purchase, while transaction fees depend on your billing plan and what you’re selling.

Tip

To sell on Squarespace, first connect a payment processing solution.

Can I use Squarespace Payments?

Review this availability guide to check if Squarespace Payments is available in your area. We’ll be rolling out Squarespace Payments to more countries soon.

Squarespace Payments rates for websites

Every time you process a transaction, you’ll be charged a fee based on the payment processing rates below. Squarespace Payments is fully integrated within the Squarespace platform, which simplifies accepting and managing payments on Squarespace. The Squarespace Payments service fees are set out below, with rates differing depending on your billing plan and the country in which your business operates. With Squarespace Payments, you can also unlock some lower payment rates with our new website billing plans.

The payment solution you use, determines your payment processing rates. You can collect and manage payments all within Squarespace with our native payment solution, Squarespace Payments. You can also connect to a third-party processor such as Stripe, PayPal, or Square.

Note

For US rates, standard card rates apply to major credit and debit cards and premium card rates apply to American Express and select business, commercial, and corporate cards. International cards incur an extra surcharge.

Instant payouts with Squarespace Payments

Service fees for the use of instant payouts with Squarespace Payments are debited from your Squarespace Payments account balance. The service fee is 1.5% of the payout amount, subject to a minimum fee depending on your location:

EU: €0.50 minimum
UK: £0.40 minimum
US: $0.50 minimum

Transaction fees

As your online store host, we collect a percentage of the total sale amount for each completed transaction. Your fee depends on your plan and what you’re selling:

Commerce

Fees for the sale of physical products, download products, service products, and donations.

These are the commerce transaction fees for our current plans:

Business	3%
Commerce Basic	0%
Commerce Advanced	0%

These are the commerce transaction fees for our plans that we're currently rolling out in waves:

Basic	2%
Core	0%
Plus	0%
Advanced	0%

Note

The Plus website plan may not be available to you on the pricing page based on your country.

These are the commerce transaction fees for our Legacy plans:

Legacy Personal	3%
Legacy Business	2%

Keep in mind:

If you signed up for Squarespace before November 3, 2015, and haven’t moved into a current billing plan, the Squarespace Commerce transaction fee doesn’t apply to you.
The Legacy Business and Legacy Personal rates apply to customers who signed up for Squarespace between November 3, 2015 and November 30, 2017, and haven’t moved into a current billing plan.
If you sell digital products, you may have different fees.
Automated tax rates

When you use automated taxes, a fee is applied to each transaction. It's combined with your total payment processing fee rather than shown as a separate line item.

Digital products

Fees for the sale of Digital Products like courses, video pages, and member sites are separate from commerce fees.

These are the digital products transaction fees for our plans that we're currently rolling out in waves:

Basic	7%
Core	5%
Plus	1%
Advanced	0%
All other plans	9% unless you have a Digital Products plan

Digital Products plans

If your site is on an older billing plan, your fees will depend on your digital products plan:

No digital products plan	9%
Starter	7%
Core	3%
Pro	0%
Invoices

Invoices are a way to send professional invoices that are personalized to your brand to your customers or clients. There are no transaction fees for invoices.

Pay Links

Transaction fees aren’t collected for standard Pay Links at checkout for Squarespace websites or for pay links for domains. 

Acuity Scheduling

You can use Acuity for booking appointments or classes. There isn't a transaction fee from Squarespace when your customer or client makes a payment for appointments. There is a payment processor fee. To learn more, visit Connecting payment processors to Acuity.

Refunds

You can provide a full or partial refund to your customers. When you refund customer orders, refund processing rates vary between payment solutions.

Squarespace Payments

If you’re subscribed to a Squarespace plan that involves transaction fees, the transaction fee is refunded to you. The Squarespace Payments rate isn't refundable.

Refunds come out of your Payments account balance, which is the total amount of payments you’ve received that day. If you don't have a balance when you initiate a refund, there will be a negative balance.

Any future payments you receive are used to balance out the negative figure. In the absence of new payments, we’ll initiate a debit from your connected bank account to cover the negative balance.
