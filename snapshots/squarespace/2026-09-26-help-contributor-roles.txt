Contributor roles and permissions
Assign roles to your contributors to manage their access to your site's content and settings.

Last updated September 17, 2026

English

When you invite a contributor, you can assign permission levels depending on your subscription. If you have multiple contributors, you can use permissions to limit their access to specific areas. You can assign multiple roles to a contributor, or make them an administrator to give them full access. Contributors can remove themselves at any time.

Also try: Managing domain permissions, Contributor basics, Inviting a contributor, Edit contributor permissions, Removing another contributor from your site, Removing yourself as a contributor, Basic authors, and Change a site owner.

Video on contributor permissions

This video shows contributor levels and the actions they can take based on their permissions.

Basic permission roles

As an owner or administrator, you can invite contributors and edit their permissions. Parking page domains only support owners and administrators.

Most Squarespace subscriptions include these basic permissions:

Owner - Each subscription has one owner, usually the person who created the account. Owners can manage all content and settings, and transfer ownership to a different account.
Administrator - Administrators can do almost everything the owner can, except transfer ownership.
Billing - These contributors can change or cancel subscriptions and view invoices. They can’t add or edit content.

Note

To let all contributors license Getty Images, turn on Let non-admins purchase assets in the Permissions & Ownership panel.

Permission roles by subscription

Some subscriptions will have unique permission roles based on the features available.

These permission roles are for Squarespace websites:

Analytics - Can access website and commerce analytics panels.
Comment moderator - Can review and post blog comments without moderation, and receive email notifications for new comments.
Email Campaign editor - Can create, send, and track analytics for Email Campaigns. They can't add or make changes to an Email Campaigns subscription.
Store manager - Can add and reorder products, manage orders, and invoice clients. They can also add some content like blog posts and events. To edit customer reviews or abandoned checkout recovery emails, store managers also need Administrator or Email Campaigns editor permissions.
Viewer - Can view private sites and password-protected sites without entering the site password. They still need passwords for specific password-protected pages.
Website editor - Can add products, blocks, and other content to existing pages. They can’t add new pages or make style changes.

If you log in to Acuity with a Squarespace account, you can assign these roles:

Scheduling administrator - Can view and manage everything in the scheduling panel.
Scheduling view & edit - Can view and edit their assigned calendars.
Scheduling view only - Can view their assigned calendars.

Note

If you log in with an Acuity account, you can assign staff the same access through a different process. Learn more about inviting contributors to Acuity Scheduling.

If you’re on an Enterprise plan, you can assign organization-level roles, and site-level permissions to the team members in your organization.

Organization roles

Team administrator - Can access, edit, and publish all organization sites, and manage member permissions at both the organization and site levels.
Team member - Can access select organization sites and create trial sites.

Site permissions

Admin - Can edit all site content and site settings.
Draft editor - Can create, edit, and save page drafts, and manage Asset library images. They can't edit live pages or publish changes.
Website editor - Can edit site content and manage Asset library images, but can’t change site settings.
Website manager - Can edit site content and page settings in the Pages panel, customize styles in the Design panel, and manage media in the Asset library.
Access for each permission level

If a permission type isn't listed, contributors with those permissions can't make changes in this part of the site.

Only the owner and administrators can make design changes to a site. This includes:

Changing the logo and title
Creating a lock screen
Creating an announcement bar
Making style changes
Switching templates (version 7.0 only)
Access to the asset library

Only the owner, administrators and website editors can access and manage the asset library. They can:

Access and use the asset library
Add content to the asset library
Delete content from the asset library
Download asset library content
Edit asset library content
Empty asset library trash
License Getty images
Organize asset library content using folders
Restore deleted content

Note

Email Campaigns editors and store managers can add files to the Asset library by uploading them to the site. However, they can’t manage files directly from the library.

Access to site analytics

Only the owner, administrators, and analytics contributors can view a site's analytics panel.

Do contributors get email notifications?

Yes. Contributors receive automated email notifications based on their role and permissions. To manage notifications, go to the Notifications tab in your contributor profile.

We send these emails to the address listed in your profile. You can review and update your email address in the Account & Security tab of your contributor profile.

Can my billing contributors disable emails?

Administrators and billing contributors receive important emails about site billing. This includes receipts for one-time purchases. Because these contain essential information, it's not possible to turn off these notifications.

If a permission type isn't listed, contributors with those permissions won't receive email notifications.
