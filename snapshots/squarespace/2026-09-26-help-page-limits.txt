Page limits
Learn about different content limitations on your Squarespace site.

Last updated January 14, 2026

English

You can add up to 1000 pages to any of our current Squarespace plans. Some page types have their own content limits. For example, we limit the number of products you can add to your store pages. These guidelines keep your site usable and fast.

This guide explains the specific page and content limits in Squarespace.

Accessing this feature

If your site is on our legacy Personal plan, it has a 20-page limit. Learn more below.

Page-specific limits

Most pages have limits for the amount of content you can add to them:

Legacy Personal plan limits

If your site is on our older, legacy Personal plan, it has a 20-page limit. To learn more about legacy plans, visit Legacy billing plans.

This section explains the pages that count or don't count toward that limit.  

Pages that count toward the limit

The pages that count toward the page limit are:

Layout pages
Album pages
Blog pages (not individual blog posts)
Calendar pages (called events pages in version 7.0)
Course pages
Cover pages
Gallery pages
Index pages and sub-pages
Portfolio pages (not individual projects)
Store pages
Project pages
Pages in the Not linked section of your navigation
Template demo pages
Videos pages
Content that doesn't count toward the limit

This content doesn't count toward the page limit:

Navigation links
Dropdowns
The default 404 error page
Individual blog posts
Individual events
Individual lessons on course pages
Individual portfolio projects
Individual product items
Individual videos on videos pages
Shopping cart
Checkout page

Note

Disabled pages count toward the general 1000-page limit, but not toward the legacy Personal plan's 20-page limit.

Suggested maximum is 400 pages

While a site can have up to 1000 pages, we don't recommend adding more than 400. Sites with this much content may load slowly, especially on mobile devices. For larger sites, it's a good idea to regularly review and delete unused content.
