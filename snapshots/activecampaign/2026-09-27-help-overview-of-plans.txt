ActiveCampaign offers different plans to help you expand and grow your business with marketing automation. These plans include the ability to drive sales, improve customer retention, and boost engagement.

With our tiered options, you can select the plan that best suits your business needs and customize it further with our SMS, Custom Reporting, Transactional Email, and Enhanced CRM add-ons. 

In this article, you’ll learn about ActiveCampaign’s pricing plans and features available. 

For specific information on costs and what features are included, please visit our pricing page. 

Plan types

Below are our plan types, including our base Email plans and WhatsApp plans. You can also combine a WhatsApp plan with a Plus, Professional, or Enterprise Email plan for a more robust experience.

Add-on packages

Depending on your base plan (outlined above), you can also purchase one of our enhanced CRM add-ons (Pipeline & Sales Engagement or Sales Engagement) and/or any of the following add-ons: SMS, Custom Reporting, and Transactional Emails.

You must have a base plan in place before purchasing an add-on package.

Add-on package name	Description	Plan(s) required for add-on
Transactional Email	

This add-on uses Postmark.

The "Send a transactional email" automation action is available with this add-on.

	Starter, Plus, Professional, and Enterprise
SMS	

With this add-on, you can send broadcast SMS campaigns and use the “Send SMS” automation action to send SMS messages to your contacts. 

You can also view replies to your SMS messages.

	Plus, Professional, and Enterprise
Custom Reports	

Create reports that give you a complete view of marketing and sales.

This will help you discover trends and areas of opportunity and grow your business with the specific data you need.
