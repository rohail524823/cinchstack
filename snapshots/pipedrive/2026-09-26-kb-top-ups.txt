Billing: Top-ups
YS
Yssel Salas
Last updated: September 2, 2026

Top-ups are paid add-ons that let you increase the limit of specific Pipedrive features.

They’re designed to give your team more flexibility, so you can scale your usage without needing to upgrade your plan.

What are top-ups?

Top-ups allow you to increase the limits of certain Pipedrive features, like reports, automations, or API tokens.

Each top-up adds a fixed number of additional units and can be purchased in multiples, up to a set maximum depending on the feature.

Users with account settings permissions enabled can manage top-ups directly in their Billing settings.

Note: Top-up prices vary depending on your billing frequency (monthly or annual). You’ll see the full cost when selecting the add-on. For more details, visit our pricing page.

How to buy top-ups

To purchase a top-up:

Go to Billing > Add-ons in your company account
Scroll to find the available top-up
Add them to your billing by activating the toggle. The add-on will turn blue once active.
Click “Review purchase” at the bottom of the page to open the subscription overview
Confirm the purchase if everything looks correct
Note: If limits haven’t yet been enforced in your account, you won’t see related top-ups available until enforcement begins.
How to remove top-ups

To remove a top-up from your subscription:

Go to Billing > Add-ons.
Click “Manage add-ons”.
Find the active top-up and untoggle the add-on. The add-on will turn grey once active.
Click “Review purchase” at the bottom of the page
Follow the checkout flow to confirm the changes
Note: Changes will take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. The checkout flow will display your renewal date before you confirm.
Top-ups vs. booster packs

While top-ups and booster packs both increase feature limits, they work differently.

If you currently use booster packs, you’ll retain access to them on your existing plan. However, you won’t be able to purchase additional ones after July 2025.

To increase limits beyond that date, you’ll need to switch to a new plan and use top-ups instead.

Note: API Tokens and Leads + Deals top-ups can still be purchased on legacy Advanced, Professional and Power plans.
Key differences

Top-ups are one standard size (no standard vs. premium)
You can buy top-ups in multiples, based on your needs
Top-ups are built specifically for the new plans
Booster packs aren’t supported on new plans and will be removed if you switch

Learn more about Pipedrive plans on our pricing page.
