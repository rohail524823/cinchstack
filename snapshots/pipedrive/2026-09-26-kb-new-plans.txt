What’s new in Pipedrive plans?
YS
Yssel Salas
Last updated: September 2, 2026

In 2025 we introduced a new pricing and packaging structure across all Pipedrive plans.

These changes reflect how the product has evolved, bringing key features and usage limits closer to how teams use Pipedrive today.

Note: Only users with account setting permissions can modify the Pipedrive account subscription.
Why did we make these changes?

Our goal is to simplify plan offerings, improve platform performance and provide a more scalable experience for businesses of all sizes.

What changed?

You’ll notice changes in both the plan names and their structure.

The new plans are:

Lite
Growth
Premium
Ultimate

Each one is designed to reflect the tools, limits and scale appropriate for that stage of business growth.

From Essential to Lite

If you’re on the Essential plan, Lite gives you added tools and higher limits with no major changes to how you work in Pipedrive.

What’s new in Lite?
Deal card customization
Choose what information shows in your pipeline view to highlight key fields, reduce clutter, and review deals faster.
Enforced two-factor authentication (2FA)
Admins can now require users to log in using 2FA, ensuring compliance and reducing security risks.
Web to mobile calls
Click any number in the web app and complete the call on your mobile device, with the call logged automatically.
Higher lead and deal limits
Essential: 2,000 active leads + deals per user
Lite: 2,500 per user
Note: If you’re above Essential’s limits, moving to Lite means you can keep your data without interruption.
How to switch from Essential to Lite?

Go to Billing > Manage subscription in your account settings.

Note: Switching to Lite will update your subscription to Pipedrive’s latest list pricing.

If you're on the Advanced plan, Growth adds tools for better forecasting, automation and lead tracking while keeping your existing workflows intact.

Forecast view (revenue by pipeline stage)
Forecast reports (project revenue over time)
Sequences
Build structured, repeatable lead-nurturing workflows using manual emails and follow-up tasks.
Contacts timeline
View a chronological record of emails, calls, and meetings to understand relationship history and personalize outreach.
Also included
Deal card customization
Enforced 2FA

From Professional to Premium

If you’re currently on the Professional plan and looking for more flexibility and advanced tools, the Premium plan offers enhanced functionality, broader limits and additional collaboration features without requiring major changes to your workflow.

What’s new in Premium?
LeadBooster included
Gain access to powerful lead generation tools:
Chatbot: Engage visitors in real time
Live Chat: Connect users to real support
Prospector: Source leads from a global database
Web Forms: Capture traffic and turn it into contacts
Custom scoring and enrichment
Automate your lead qualification process with:
Scoring based on deal fit
Enrichment using firmographic data (e.g., industry, revenue)
Team inbox
Collaborate on email conversations using shared inboxes like sales@yourcompany.com while maintaining visibility and ownership.
Also included
Projects: Manage post-sale tasks inside Pipedrive
Enforced 2FA: Ensure account-wide login security

Why are limits being introduced?

These limits reflect industry best practices and are designed to:

Keep the platform stable and performant
Ensure consistent usability across all accounts
Prevent excessive configuration that could reduce overall system reliability

The goal is to make sure Pipedrive remains efficient and scalable for everyone.

Note: Ultimate is generally priced lower than Enterprise. For current rates, see our pricing page.

In addition, we’ve made our lead generation add-on included on the Ultimate plan, giving you access to:

Chatbot: Engage visitors in real time
Live Chat: Connect users to real support

Web Forms: Capture traffic and turn it into contacts
