How does pricing work in Pipedrive?
JT
Jenny Takahara
Last updated: September 2, 2026
Note: Only users with account settings access rights can manage Pipedrive billing. Learn more about user management in this article.

Pipedrive offers four pricing plans to fit teams of any size: Lite, Growth, Premium and Ultimate. You can further customize your subscription with optional add-ons and top-ups.

To view and edit your current subscription, open the account menu in the top-right corner and go to Billing.

Pipedrive plans and billing cycles

To change your plan, click “Manage subscription”, then select “All plans” to see all available options.

Once you’ve chosen your plan, select your preferred billing cycle in the same view:

Here’s how the billing cycles differ:

You can enhance your plan with optional add-ons that provide access to additional tools like prospecting, document management and email marketing. Add-ons are billed according to the billing cycle of your selected plan.

What affects your total subscription cost

Your total cost depends on a few key factors:

Selected plan – Each plan (Lite, Growth, Premium, Ultimate) has its own base price per seat
Billing cycle – You can choose between annual or monthly billing
Number of seats – You’re charged per seat, whether it’s assigned to a user or not. Learn more about seats and users.
Add-ons and top-ups – Optional features and usage extensions that follow your plan's billing cycle
Currency and location – Your location determines the billing currency and available payment methods

For example, if you’re on the Growth annual plan with four seats and the LeadBooster add-on, your total would be:

4 seats × Growth annual plan price + LeadBooster add-on price
