Seamless handoffs with CRM project management software

Project planning shouldn’t start from zero once a deal is won. With traditional setups, sales and delivery live in different tools, forcing teams to rebuild context and increasing the risk of missed details. A CRM with project management keeps everything connected from the start – without relying on integrations or manual handovers. No switching tools, no switching tabs.

Projects by Pipedrive is CRM and project management software built to take your work seamlessly from sale to delivery. As soon as a deal closes, you can generate an AI-assisted project brief using your deal's emails, notes and files – it’ll just need to be reviewed and confirmed by your team before delivery.

Run delivery from the same platform you used to close the deal

Unlike standalone project management tools that operate separately from your CRM, Pipedrive keeps sales, delivery and customer context in one place. The minute you close a deal, Pipedrive can use deal context to auto-populate your project brief. Plan timelines, assign tasks and collaborate with your team while keeping all customer information – commitments, notes and files – straight from your sales history. Net result? You can quickly generate projects using deal context so you don't have to start from scratch – that means fast automated handovers with zero context or information lost.

Catch problems before they escalate

Most project management tools only alert you once tasks are overdue – when it’s often too late to prevent delays. Without clear visibility, small issues can quickly turn into missed deadlines and unhappy clients.

With Projects by Pipedrive, you stay ahead by linking project progress to what matters most: revenue, not just task completion. Gantt timelines (beta), task dependencies and AI-powered health summaries (beta) help you spot risks early, see what’s slipping and act before delivery or your pipeline is affected.

Instead of reacting to problems, your team can focus on the work that keeps projects – and your pipeline – on track.

Standardize delivery from the moment a deal is won. Automatically launch projects with pre-built workflows – tasks, timelines, dependencies and milestones – all tied to the original deal context.

With automations and bulk project management, you can deliver a consistent, high-quality experience to every client – without separate systems for sales and delivery, no matter how fast your pipeline grows.

CRM project management software FAQ
What is a CRM project management software?

CRM project management software is a unified workspace that connects sales and delivery in one system. Instead of moving work into separate tools after a deal is won, your team can continue execution with full context – including deal history, notes, emails and commitments – already in place. This eliminates handoff gaps, reduces manual setup and ensures delivery starts exactly where sales left off, leading to faster onboarding and more consistent outcomes.

How to choose a CRM project management software?

Choose a solution that closes the gap between “deal won” and “delivery” without requiring integrations or manual handovers. The best platforms don’t just track tasks – they connect delivery to revenue and customer context. Look for features like automated deal-to-project handover, shared visibility across teams and insights into project health, so you can spot risks early and prioritize work that impacts your pipeline. A strong solution should help your team execute what was sold, not rebuild it.

What are the benefits of a CRM project management software?

The biggest benefit is continuity – your team can move from sale to delivery without losing context or switching tools. This creates a single source of truth for both your team and your client, reducing miscommunication and eliminating the need to recreate project briefs. It also helps you standardize delivery with repeatable workflows and automations, so you can scale operations while maintaining a consistent, high-quality customer experience.

What features to look for in a CRM project management software?

Look for features that support the full lifecycle from sale to delivery. This includes automated handover from deals to projects, so your team starts with complete context, as well as planning tools like timelines, dependencies and milestones to manage execution. Strong collaboration features – such as shared tasks, @-mentions and client visibility – help keep everyone aligned. Finally, reporting and project health insights ensure you can track progress, identify risks early and keep delivery connected to revenue.

How much does a CRM with project management features cost?

The cost of a CRM with project management features depends on the level of functionality and scale your team needs. While standalone tools may appear cheaper, using separate systems for sales and delivery often leads to inefficiencies and added costs over time. A unified CRM with project management helps reduce tool sprawl, improve team productivity and protect revenue by ensuring smoother delivery. Many providers, including Pipedrive, offer free trials so you can evaluate the value before committing to a plan.
