Billing
Note: This action is only available to users with account settings access.

When adding the Campaigns add-on to your billing, select the tier that better fits your needs.

Click on “Change number” to change the number of subscribers.

As you move the slider, the estimated cost will appear on the right-hand side. The more contacts you add, the less you pay per subscriber.

In the checkout section, you’ll be asked to confirm the total cost, consisting of your campaigns add-on tier and your Pipedrive subscription, as well as the billing date and amount charged.

Once the purchase is confirmed, you’ll be able to set up your campaigns.
