Frequently asked questions
How does Pipedrive Nova use my data?

Nova securely analyzes your Pipedrive data and transcripts to deliver tailored meeting prep, recaps, and CRM updates. We only use your data to power your experience. We don’t allow third-party AI providers to train on your data, nor do we train our own models on it without your explicit permission.

Can I choose which meetings Pipedrive Nova joins or remove it from a call?

Yes, you have complete control over which calls Nova captures. You can easily enable or disable the auto-join feature at any time within Settings. When using the Companion desktop app, you decide whether to capture meetings or not.

Why not just use MCP or my custom AI assistant instead of Pipedrive Nova?

MCP and AI assistants are reactive. They answer questions using whatever’s already in your CRM and only when you ask. Nova is proactive. It builds your pre-call brief, captures the conversation and drafts the CRM update automatically, no prompting needed.

Use MCP for on-demand queries or custom reporting. Use Nova for meeting prep and CRM updates to be handled automatically. Many teams use both together.

Do I need consent to record meetings with Pipedrive Nova?

As the meeting organizer, you’re responsible and accountable for ensuring compliance with applicable laws and regulations, including informing participants that Nova is active and obtaining their consent where required by law, and respecting any participant's request not to be recorded. Nova helps you stay compliant by offering pre-meeting email notifications with built-in consent or opt-out options. Additionally, Nova can generate a shareable consent link, and when the bot joins a call, it automatically posts an in-meeting chat message notifying all participants.

Meeting hosts can remove the Nova bot from the call at any time, which will stop the recording.

Recording and transcription laws vary by jurisdiction. Several U.S. states and the EU require consent from participants before any recording or transcription begins. Some require consent from every participant, others require only one party's consent. You are accountable for honoring participants’ preferences.

Will Pipedrive Nova change my Pipedrive CRM data without my permission?

No. Nova may add meeting summaries and transcripts as notes, but it won't change existing CRM data without your approval.

What is conversation intelligence software?

Conversation intelligence software captures and analyzes sales calls to surface insights like next steps, deal risks and buyer sentiment. Nova is built natively into Pipedrive, so those insights become CRM updates instead of a separate report.

What are the benefits of conversation intelligence software?

The biggest wins from conversation intelligence software are fewer admin tasks and fewer missed details. Nova automatically recaps every call and drafts the CRM update, so nothing falls through the cracks.

How does conversation intelligence software work for sales teams?

Conversation intelligence software records or transcribes sales conversations, then uses AI to pull out details like next steps, objections and deal updates. Nova matches those details to your Pipedrive fields, so reps can keep, edit or skip the CRM update after every call.

How to choose the best conversation intelligence software?

The best conversation intelligence software fits seamlessly into your existing workflow, rather than adding another app to manage. Look for tools that live inside your CRM, turn calls into reviewable updates and give you control over what gets captured. Nova is built natively into Pipedrive to do exactly that.

How can I integrate conversation intelligence software with my current CRM?

Most conversation intelligence software connects to your CRM through custom integrations or manual field mapping, which takes setup and upkeep. Nova skips that step. It’s built natively into Pipedrive, so call insights and CRM updates already live in the same place.
