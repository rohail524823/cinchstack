Choose a Workspace plan

Learn about available Workspace plans and choose a plan according to your team’s needs.
A Workspace is a space where you stage sites — and where you can invite teammates and clients to work on sites together. Upgrading your Workspace plan gives you access to enhanced staging capabilities and advanced collaboration features. Pick a plan based on what your team wants to accomplish. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan anytime as your needs evolve.
Visit webflow.com/pricing for a detailed breakdown of plans and pricing. Below, we’ll provide an overview.
Compare each Workspace plan
All Workspace plans include:

- 1 full seat for the Workspace owner

- Ability to add up to 100 free Reviewer seats and purchase unlimited full or limited seats

- Ability to have unlimited hosted sites (i.e., sites with paid plans)

- A set number of AI credits

Starter Workspace
The Starter Workspace is provided to everyone who signs up for Webflow for free and includes:

- Two webflow.io unhosted sites (i.e., sites without a paid plan used for staging)

- Two static pages per site

- 50 CMS items per site

- Ability to invite two Agency or Freelancer guests

How to review your current Workspace plan
To view your current Workspace plan:

- Open the Workspace for which you’d like to view your plan

- Click Plans

- Click the For freelancers & agencies tab to review those plans

There, you can review all Workspace plan features and upgrade or downgrade your Workspace plan.

Important

Each Workspace supports one credit card on file, which will be used for all Workspace plans, Site plans (i.e., site hosting), Seats, and add-ons purchased within that Workspace. You can update the Workspace’s credit card before making a purchase to pay for something on another card. If you have multiple Workspaces, each Workspace may have a unique credit card on file.
