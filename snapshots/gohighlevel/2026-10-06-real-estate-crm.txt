What is HighLevel?

HighLevel is an AI-powered sales, marketing and automation platform built for agencies. It’s designed to replace multiple disconnected tools with a single, scalable solution so you can manage leads, automate follow-ups, build funnels and close clients from one dashboard.

What tools does HighLevel replace?

HighLevel can replace your CRM, funnel builder, email marketing platform, SMS tool, scheduling software, reputation management system and automation platform. Tools like HubSpot, ClickFunnels, Calendly, Mailchimp and Zapier can often be consolidated into one login.

Can I white-label HighLevel and sell it as my own platform?

Yes. On the Agency Pro plan you can fully white-label HighLevel with your logo, your domain and your pricing. Your clients see your brand and you build recurring SaaS revenue without writing a single line of code.

How does HighLevel help me manage multiple client accounts?

One dashboard gives you access to every client. Each sub-account keeps data separate and secure. You can clone funnels, duplicate workflows and switch between accounts instantly.
