Pricing plans
Plan features

There are several plans to help your business grow at every stage. Each Shopify pricing plan offers a variety of features. There is no one specific plan that works for every business, but you can use this guide to help you decide which plan works for you.

If you have a retail store and need hardware such as a cash register, a barcode scanner, or a receipt printer, then consider Shopify POS.
