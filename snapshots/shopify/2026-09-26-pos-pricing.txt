What's POS Pro?
Every paid plan location comes with casual in-person selling features—upgrade to POS Pro to get a more powerful, enhanced feature set.
Staff management
Get unlimited staff accounts, track performance, and control access to sensitive information with permissions.
Inventory management
Perform inventory counts, complete purchase orders, and get low stock alerts from a single place.
Clienteling
Empower staff to create personalized shopping experiences with tools to track customer preferences.
Customer profiles
Capture rich customer data in store and online and access lifetime value reporting and loyalty insights directly in POS.
Retail analytics
Monitor stores in real time from a central location and get holistic insights across your entire business.
