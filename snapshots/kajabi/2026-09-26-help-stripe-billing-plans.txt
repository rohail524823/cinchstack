Understanding Stripe's updated billing plans for Kajabi Heroes

Review Stripe’s billing plan changes and how they affect Kajabi Payments fees.

​
Overview
On July 10, 2024, Stripe updated their Billing plans and structure, which introduced an adjustment in their subscription fee from 0.5% to 0.7%. For more details, review Stripe’s documentation on these updates. For specific questions regarding Stripe’s fee changes, we recommend contacting Stripe directly, as they can provide the most accurate information about their direct pricing.
As Kajabi Payments is built in partnership with Stripe, we also adjusted our subscription and payment plan fees from 0.5% to 0.7% effective August 14, 2024.
To honor Heroes who supported Kajabi Payments from the beginning, we offered a promotional rate of 0.5% to all Heroes who set up Kajabi Payments by August 14, 2024. This promotional rate remained in effect until June 30, 2025, after which the pricing applies.
​
FAQs
​
How does this affect Kajabi Heroes who use Stripe?
Heroes on a Stripe Billing Starter or Billing Scale plan before July 10th, 2024 received promotional pricing of 0.5% until June 30th, 2025, after which the 0.7% pricing applies. For specific questions about Stripe’s fee changes, contact Stripe directly as they can provide the most accurate information about their direct pricing.
​
I have custom Billing pricing with Stripe, will that still be honored?
According to Stripe’s documentation, if you have a custom Billing sales contract, there will be no changes to the current agreement. For more questions regarding specific pricing, contact Stripe directly.
