A few things people ask before they start.

‍

Which plan should I start on?

If you haven't sold anything yet, Basic is the right plan for you. Growth makes sense once revenue is coming in and your list is past a few thousand people. Pro is for anyone running more than one brand, or who wants their own app in the App Store.

What happens when my trial ends?

You move onto the plan you chose, and you can change it any time. Upgrade, downgrade, or go from monthly to annual at any time.

Does Kajabi take a cut of my sales?

Kajabi does not take a revenue share as a platform, you keep whatever you earn. We do have a standard credit card processing fee for Kajabi Payments, which differs by country. All fees available by country here. Additionally, if you are in a region where Kajabi Payments is offered but opt to use third party payment processor, there is an additional processing fee. If you are in a region where Kajabi Payments is not offered, there is no additional processing fee.

Isn't Kajabi expensive for a course platform?

If you are just looking at a course delivery platform, yes. But, Kajabi is also the email tool, the website, the community and the checkout, and it takes no cut of your sales. Price the pieces à la carte and Kajabi is the cheaper way to run the whole business from a few thousand contacts up.

What happens when I hit a plan limit?

We'll let you know, and you can either add capacity to your current plan or move up to the next one. Contacts come in blocks of 25,000 for $125 a month, at any size.

Can I change or cancel my plan later?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel whenever you like, and every plan has a 30-day money-back guarantee.
