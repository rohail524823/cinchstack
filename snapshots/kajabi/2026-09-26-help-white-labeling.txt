Is white labeling possible?

Learn about Kajabi branding removal options available on Growth and Pro plans.

While it’s not possible to completely remove all Kajabi branding, with Growth and Pro plans, you can remove the “Powered by Kajabi” branding just below the page footer of your website.
However, transactional emails (i.e. auto-generated, non-marketing emails) and the Kajabi mobile app display Kajabi branding that cannot currently be removed. Branding in all other areas of Kajabi can be customized.
