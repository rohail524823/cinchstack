Migrate a website and content to Kajabi

Move your website design, pages, and content from another platform or a different Kajabi site, since Kajabi does not convert designs automatically.

Kajabi does not currently offer free in-house migration services from other platforms, and moving a website from another platform does not automatically convert its design. If you are migrating content from an outside platform, or moving content from one Kajabi site to another, the process is manual.
​
Move a website from another platform
Your existing website design and formatting does not transfer automatically. You will need to manually rebuild your content using Kajabi’s Website Builder, including its Sections and Blocks. Learn how to set up your site with Kajabi.
If you want to transfer your existing customers to Kajabi, you will need to import them as contacts. Learn how to import contacts into Kajabi.
Tip: If you want to keep your third-party website active while using Kajabi for your Products and checkout, you do not have to migrate your whole site at once. Link your existing website to your new Kajabi Pages using buttons, CTAs, or your navigation menu, so visitors can move between your original site and your Kajabi Pages. Learn how to copy a page link.

FAQs
​
Will my website design convert automatically when I move to Kajabi?
No. Kajabi does not convert an existing site’s design or formatting automatically, regardless of the platform you are moving from. You will rebuild your pages using Kajabi’s Website Builder.
​
How is my new Kajabi website connected to my Courses, Coaching, Community, Newsletter, or Podcast?
Your website and its Pages are separate from your Products. Products hold your actual content, while your Pages link visitors to the Offers that unlock those Products. See how Kajabi content connects for a full breakdown of how these pieces fit together.
