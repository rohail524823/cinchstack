Our mission

We believe the next decade belongs to real experts, people with earned wisdom, real experience, and something worth teaching. In a world flooded with AI-generated noise, trust has become the new currency.

‍

Kajabi exists to give these experts leverage. A platform that amplifies their humanity, protects their credibility, and turns their hard-earned expertise into income, impact, and independence.

Building this platform requires a team that cares deeply about craft, speed, and the people we serve. We're looking for people who want to do the best work of their careers, not check boxes.

‍

If you believe software should feel human, that details matter, and that real expertise deserves better tools, we'd love to hear from you.

Our values

The principles that guide how we build and how we work together.

Hero obsessed

Our Heroes' success is our top priority. Every decision starts and ends with them.

Ship to learn

We move fast because our Heroes don't have time to wait. Progress beats perfection.

Own the outcome

When it's your task, you own the result. Titles don't solve problems — people do.

Candor over comfort

Feedback is a gift. We debate fiercely, decide decisively, and commit completely.

Craft matters

We sweat the details others skip. Quality isn't a phase — it's how we build.

Stronger together

No egos, no passengers. We pull in the same direction because our Heroes depend on us.
