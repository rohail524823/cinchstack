Kajabi Payments Processing Fees

Understand the processing fees associated with Kajabi Payments for each supported region.

​
Overview
When you use Kajabi Payments to process your payments, you’ll be charged a processing fee for every successful transaction. Kajabi charges an industry-standard processing fee competitive with other major payment processors.
By choosing Kajabi Payments as your payment processor, you not only gain the advantage of competitive processing fees but also provide your customers with the convenience of paying through additional payment methods such as a digital wallet like Apple Pay or Google Pay, or via a Buy Now Pay Later solution such as Afterpay during checkout.
Note: Kajabi Payments is currently available to Kajabi Heroes whose businesses are registered in the Australia, Canada, Select European Countries, the United Arab Emirates, the United Kingdom, and the United States.

You may qualify for discounted card rates based on your plan. To check, head to Account Settings › Billing to see the details of your current plan.
Curious about how your plan stacks up against our plans and where you might save more? Visit our Pricing Hub to explore.
​
View individual fees
You can find a breakdown of the individual fees for each transaction on the Payouts Page.
Navigate to Payments from your Kajabi Account
Click View payouts
Click All payouts
Click into the specified payout date
Fees can also be viewed from the customer purchase info page:
From the Kajabi Dashboard, select Payments
From Payments, click Transactions
Select the Customer transaction you wish to view individual fees for
​
Third-party payment processing fee
If you process card payments through a third-party processor connected directly to your account, rather than through Kajabi Payments, a transaction fee applies in addition to that processor’s own fees. The fee is based on your Kajabi plan — see the pricing page for current rates. PayPal is not subject to this fee.
This fee applies only in countries where Kajabi Payments is available. If Kajabi Payments isn’t offered in your country, no third-party transaction fee is charged.
