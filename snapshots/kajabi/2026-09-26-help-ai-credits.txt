Billing
AI Credits

Understand how AI Credits work on Kajabi and what your plan includes for transcription, translation, and dubbing.

AI Credits power transcription, translation, and dubbing across Kajabi. Instead of separate minute buckets, you get a unified pool of credits to use across any AI video service.
​
Plans that include AI Credits
Plan	Monthly AI Credits (reset on the 1st of the month)
Pro & Founders	1,000 credits
Growth	500 credits
Note: All plans are able to purchase AI credits by going to Account Settings → Subscription.
​
Credit-to-minute conversion
Credits are consumed at different rates depending on the complexity and cost of the service.
Transcription: 1 credit = 1 minute
Translation: 10 credits = 1 minute
Dubbing: 100 credits = 1 minute
This structure ensures predictable usage while giving you full flexibility on how to apply your credits each month.
​
Example credit usage
With 1,000 credits per month, you can choose any combination of:
Service	Credit cost	Minutes
Transcription	1 credit / min	1,000 minutes
Translation	10 credits / min	100 minutes
Dubbing	100 credits / min	10 minutes
Or mix and match to fit your needs:
600 minutes transcription + 40 minutes translation + 3 minutes dubbing
900 minutes transcription + 1 minute dubbing
Any combination totaling 1,000 credits
​
Purchase additional credits
There are two options when you are looking to purchase additional credits:
Monthly bundles: Add 500 credits to your monthly allotment for $15/month. Note that unused bundle credits do not roll over at the end of each billing cycle.
One-time top-up: Purchase 500 credits for a flat $15 fee. This is a great option if you only need a temporary boost without committing to a monthly increase.
​
FAQs
​
Do transcriptions use AI Credits?
Yes. Transcriptions require AI Credits and are a required step before you can use translations or dubbing.
​
Can I upload my own transcript?
Yes. You can upload your own transcript and review it before moving forward. This allows you to confirm accuracy and formatting before using AI Credits. This can help save AI Credits and give you more control over accuracy.
​
When are AI Credits considered used?
AI Credits are considered spent as soon as they’re used to complete an action, such as transcription, translation, or dubbing. Used credits can’t be refunded. If a transcription, translation, or dub fails during generation, the credits will not be used.
​
Which language should I translate or dub first?
Start with your most requested or highest-impact language to maximize value from your AI Credits.
​
Is there a video length limit?
Yes. Your video length must fit within the AI Credits available on your account. The maximum upload size for Kajabi is 4GB.
​
What happens if my video is longer than my available credits?
If your video exceeds your available credits, you won’t be able to start transcription, translation, or dubbing until additional credits are purchased or become available at the start of the month.
​
When do AI Credits reset?
AI Credits reset on the 1st of every month (PST).
​
Can I purchase more credits?
Yes, additional credits may be purchased in a bundle of 500 credits for $15/month or as a one time purchase of 500 credits for $15.
​
Is there a way to test before spending credits?
Yes. Uploading and reviewing a transcript first allows you to confirm everything looks right before using credits on translation or dubbing.
