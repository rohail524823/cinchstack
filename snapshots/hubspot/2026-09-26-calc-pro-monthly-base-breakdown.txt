Marketing SMS Long Code Add-On

$75/mo

Purchase required to use SMS for marketing. Includes SMS setup, maintenance, and 1,000 message segments per month, effective on your contract start date. Unused message segments don’t roll over into the following month. Sending is only available from U.S.-based phone numbers, and messages can only be sent to U.S./Canada (+1) phone numbers. Both incoming and outgoing SMS messages count against your message segment limit. As applicable, incoming MMS messages are counted as 5 message segments each.
