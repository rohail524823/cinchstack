Creator vs Pro: choose the right plan | Kit Help Center
The Creator and Pro plans are paid plans that include Kit's full set of core email marketing tools. Pro adds advanced reporting, automation, audience, and growth features.

 Pro features
 Collaborative editing
 Work together on Broadcasts with your team members in real time with no locking or waiting for someone else to finish first. See who else is editing via the profile icons at the top of the editor.
 Learn more about collaborative editing 
 Advanced deliverability reporting
 View open and click data broken down by email provider (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook) or by list segment. Use this data to identify deliverability issues and adjust your content strategy.
 Learn more about advanced deliverability reporting 
 Subscriber engagement analytics
 See which subscribers are most engaged, where they came from, and how long they've stayed on your list. Use this to double down on the channels and content that are working.
 Learn more about subscriber engagement analytics 
 Subscriber engagement scoring
 Each subscriber receives a score from 1 to 5 stars based on how they engage with your emails and products. Filter and segment by score to target your most active subscribers or clean inactive contacts from your list.
 Learn more about subscriber scoring 
 Facebook Custom Audiences sync
 Connect Kit Segments to Facebook ad audiences. Subscribers are added and removed automatically as they move in and out of segments, so your ad targeting stays current without manual CSV uploads.
 Learn more about Facebook Custom Audiences 
 Edit links in sent Broadcasts
 If a Broadcast contains a broken or incorrect URL, you can update it after sending. Open the Broadcast's Clicks report and replace the link, and all future clicks will go to the updated URL.
 Learn how to change a URL after sending 
 Unlimited team members
 Add as many team members or assistants as you need and switch between accounts from a single dropdown. The Creator plan is limited to two users.
 Learn how to invite your team 
 Newsletter referral system (SparkLoop)
 Set up a referral program that rewards subscribers for bringing in new readers, or pay partners per signup. Subscriber data syncs between Kit and SparkLoop automatically.
 Learn how to set up a referral program 
 Insights dashboard
 Track acquisition, engagement, sales, and list growth in one place. Requires a tracking pixel and UTM tracking to be enabled. Use this dashboard to see which Broadcasts and traffic sources are driving revenue.
 Learn how to set up Insights 
 Subscriber Signals
 Get automatic audience research on the people behind your email addresses, including their demographics and social influence. Use these insights to tailor content and pitch sponsors with confidence.
 Learn more about Subscriber Signals 
 How to switch between Creator and Pro
 You can upgrade or downgrade anytime by going to Settings in the navigation > Account and Billing . Changes take effect immediately.
 To upgrade to Pro: 
 Go to your Account and Billing settings
 Under My Plan click on Add to plan
