Why use Composer instead of a general-purpose AI tool?
Composer is built for marketing and works directly in Klaviyo. It uses your customer data, brand context, campaign history, and marketing performance to deliver more relevant analysis, recommendations, and content—and helps you turn those insights into action without leaving Klaviyo.
