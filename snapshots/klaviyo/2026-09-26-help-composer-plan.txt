How usage is measured

Every time Composer does work for you, it uses some capacity.

Usage on Composer is dynamic. The same action can cost different amounts depending on the request size, complexity, and data, so two campaigns or two audits won't always cost exactly the same. We show estimated ranges, not fixed costs.

Some questions are free. Asking Composer for support or general information does not use any credits.

Our Paid Composer Plan ($20) offers 4X capacity compared to our Free Plan.

Why costs vary

Three things drive how much capacity an action uses:

Request complexity: A quick check costs less than a deep, multi-part request.
How much data Composer reviews: Auditing one short message uses fewer capacity than analyzing a large campaign with many components.
The type of action: Generating a full campaign does more work than answering a focused question, so it costs more.

Because of this, treat the numbers above as estimated ranges, not fixed costs.

Track your usage

You can see your usage any time in the in-product Composer usage panel. It shows how far into your free access you are and how many credits you've used. Credit usage appears here after your actions run, so you can keep an eye on your balance as you go.

Your free plan

As a Klaviyo customer, you automatically get a free Composer plan with some capacity to start. There's nothing to sign up for, opt into, or activate, and no credit card is needed to start.

Your free plan capacity restarts at the beginning of your usage or billing cycle. If you run out of capacity or hit 100% usage, you can upgrade to a paid Composer plan or wait until your next billing cycle begins.

Composer lets you know as you approach your limit so there are no surprises. You'll get alerts when you reach 75%, 90%, and 100% of your plan capacity.

Upgrading to a paid plan

You can upgrade your Composer plan at any time to our $20 plan. This $20 plan includes 4X more capacity than your free plan.

Composer lets you know as you approach your limit so there are no surprises. You'll get alerts when you reach 75%, 90%, and 100% of your plan capacity. Like the free plan, capacity restarts at the beginning of your usage or billing cycle. If you run out of capacity or hit 100% usage on your paid Composer plan, you can always get more by turning on Flexible Overages.

A Flexible Overage is a one-time purchase that includes a set amount of additional capacity units at a set unit rate. For Composer, additional Composer usage can be purchased in $10 increments. When you opt in to flexible overages, you will incur the full $10 charge at the time of usage, regardless of how much additional capacity you need to use. If you need additional capacity, you may incur multiple flex overage charges based on the actions you are taking inside of Composer.

We recommend opting in to Flexible Overages when you first purchase the plan to make sure you are never stopped or blocked from using Composer when you need it most.

You can opt out of Flexible overages any time by navigating to Settings > Billing > Preferences.
