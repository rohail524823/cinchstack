Upgrading & Downgrading
Changing your plan

To change plans, click your account name, click Billing, and click Change plan on the Plans tab. See also How to add or change a plan in Klaviyo.

The Change Plan button only appears if you have Billing access, are not purely manually billed, and can modify account-family plans. If you're on a contract, you won't see this button; instead, you'll see a notice that your account is on a contract, prompting you to contact your Klaviyo representative.

Upgrading

When you upgrade (either manually or automatically), it takes effect immediately (when the limit is crossed for auto-upgrade, or right away at checkout for a manual upgrade). You then stay on that plan until you upgrade again or downgrade.

When you upgrade, you'll be charged the difference between your current plan and the new plan for the initial month, and then the full amount of the new plan in subsequent months.

You cannot upgrade if there's an outstanding invoice in your account.

Downgrading

When downgrading:

You must have fewer active profiles than the plan you want to downgrade to. Klaviyo won't let you select a plan that's too small for your actual active profile count, even right after a big cleanup (see the 72-hour refresh note above).
It does not take effect immediately: the change is typically scheduled for your next billing cycle.
If you upgrade before the downgrade takes effect, it cancels the scheduled downgrade.
Klaviyo does not offer refunds for mid-cycle downgrades.
You cannot downgrade if there is an outstanding invoice for your account.

You can confirm which happened (upgrade vs. downgrade, and what triggered it) by checking the invoice reason on the Payment & Invoices tab's Plan change history.

Does changing my plan reset my billing date?

No. Upgrading or downgrading never changes your billing date. The only way to reset it is to cancel your account and later resubscribe, which starts a new billing date from scratch.

When do billing plans renew?

All Klaviyo plans renew automatically on a monthly basis. The exact date depends on your personal billing cycle:

Free plans always renew on the first of the month.
Paid plans renew on the date your account began to pay for Klaviyo.

You're billed in advance for the upcoming cycle, not for the cycle that just ended, so your invoice covers the month ahead.

To view when your billing cycle renews:

Click your organization name in the lower-left corner.
Click Billing.
Review the dates for your cycle, which are at the top of the page in the Overview tab.

Beginning on October 8, 2025, Klaviyo aligned customers’ usage cycles with their billing cycle. Your billing cycle is based on when you first signed up for a paid plan with Klaviyo, which you can view on the Billing Overview page. Historically, your usage cycle ended at midnight on that same day. With this new streamlined approach, your usage cycle and billing cycle will share the same date and time. Only the time is changing, the billing date remains the same, and there is no action needed on your part.

Your billing date remains the same and there is nothing that you need to do.

Managing payment methods and failed payments
Add or change your payment method

To update the card used for your Klaviyo subscription:

Go to Billing from the menu in the bottom-left corner of your account.
In the billing settings, add a new card or payment method.
Set the new card as your default payment method if you want future charges to use it.

If you manage multiple Klaviyo accounts, repeat this in each account where you want to change the card.

What happens when a payment fails

If a payment fails (for example, due to insufficient funds, card expiry, or a bank/KYC block):

Klaviyo will automatically retry the charge according to an internal retry schedule.
You may see notices in your account and receive billing-related emails prompting you to update your payment method.
If payment is not successful after repeated attempts, your plan may become restricted or your account may be temporarily locked until the issue is resolved.

After you update your card, Klaviyo will usually attempt billing again automatically on the next scheduled retry.

Why the "Retry payment" button may be greyed out

In some cases you may see a Retry payment button on the Billing page that is disabled (greyed out). Common reasons include:

There is no outstanding invoice that can be retried yet.
Klaviyo is already in the process of retrying the payment automatically.
Your account is on a specific billing arrangement where manual retries are not available.

If the button remains unavailable after you have added a valid card and waited for a retry window, contact Support for help and include your account email and recent invoice ID.

Card blocked, KYC checks, or plan locked due to failed payments

If your bank or provider has blocked your card due to KYC (know your customer) checks or another compliance reason:

Add a new, verified card in Billing and set it as your default payment method.
Ask your bank to confirm that online and international recurring payments to Klaviyo are allowed.

If your plan is locked or your account shows outstanding debt that you cannot clear via self-service:

Take a screenshot of your Billing page, including any error message.
Collect your latest invoice ID and account email.
Contact Support so they can review and advise on next steps.

Profile and Email (The Klaviyo Platform Plan)

Profile and Email plans are based on both the number of:

Active profiles in your account.
Emails sent during the current billing cycle

Your billing plan must cover the number of active profiles in your account. If not, you will be moved to a profile-compliant plan as of the next billing cycle. To avoid changing plans due to profile enforcement, you will need to reduce your active profile count to be within your current plan's limits at least 24 hours before your next billing cycle.

Your Profile and Email plan must be compliant with the number of active profiles you have at the end of your billing cycle. This means that if you have 7,000 Active Profiles, your plan must allow for at least that amount. You will be automatically upgraded to a profile-compliant plan at the end of each billing cycle, if you exceed your level of profile usage.

Your emails can be to any of your active profiles. You can send an equal amount across all of your profiles (10 to each), or send more to certain groups and less to others (e.g., 100 to group A, and 1 to group B).

What are active profiles?

Klaviyo charges for any messages that leave the system: any received or bounced emails count toward your plan limit, but skipped emails do not.

How profile counts affect your bill

Klaviyo bills by billable profiles, which are the profiles that are still eligible to receive marketing from you.

Billable profiles: Active profiles that can receive at least one type of marketing (e.g., have a valid email address or phone number and are not suppressed or deleted).
Suppressed or unsubscribed profiles: Profiles that you have marked as no longer marketable (e.g., unsubscribed, bounced, or manually suppressed). These profiles remain in your account for historical reporting, but they are not included in your billable profile count once they are fully processed as suppressed.
Deleted profiles: Profiles that you permanently remove from your account. Deleted profiles do not count toward your billable profile total going forward and cannot be recovered.

Your monthly profile tier is based on the total number of billable profiles in your account at the time of billing. Note that after you suppress, delete, or bulk-remove profiles, it can take up to 72 hours for your active profile count to fully refresh; see Understanding your account usage for what to expect during that window and how the plan picker protects you from selecting a plan that's too small.

Double opt-in (confirmation) emails don't count toward your email send usage, regardless of your plan.

When suppression or deletion changes your billing tier

If you suppress or delete a large number of profiles, your billable profile count will decrease after Klaviyo finishes processing those changes. Once your billable profiles fall below the threshold for your current tier, you can select a lower plan.

For example:

If you currently pay for a plan up to 210,000 profiles and you suppress or delete enough profiles so that only 50,000 remain billable, you become eligible to move to the tier that covers up to 50,000 profiles.
Your plan does not automatically downgrade when you reduce profiles; you must choose a lower tier in the Billing page after your billable profile count has updated.
Steps to reduce profiles and downgrade your plan
Review your current profile count
Go to your account's billing or usage area to see how many profiles are currently billable.

2. Suppress or delete profiles you no longer need

Suppress contacts you do not plan to market to (e.g., inactive leads, bounced addresses, or contacts that should not receive campaigns).
Delete profiles you want to remove permanently from Klaviyo (for example, test data or contacts imported in error). Be careful: deletion cannot be undone.

3. Wait for your billable profile count to update

Klaviyo updates usage several times per day. After your changes are processed, check your billable profile total again.

4. Select a lower billing tier

When your billable profiles fall into a lower tier, go to Billing in your account and choose a plan that matches your new profile level.

You MUST downgrade more than 24 hours before your billing/usage cycle turns over to avoid monthly charges. If you downgrade within 24 hours of your billing/usage cycle, it will not take effect.

Profiles without email addresses or phone numbers

Some accounts contain profiles created from other systems (e.g., an ERP) that do not include email addresses or phone numbers.

If these profiles are not marketable and you do not need them for reporting, you can delete them to reduce clutter and potentially lower your billable profile count.
If you want to keep them for historical data but not pay to market to them, suppress them so they no longer count as billable once suppression is fully applied.

Always confirm your new billable profile count in the billing or usage view before changing your plan so that you select the most appropriate tier.

Mobile Messaging

You pay upfront for a monthly Mobile Messaging subscription based on the amount of spend you think you need i.e. $15 or $25. The Mobile Messaging plan that is best for your account is dependent entirely on how many mobile messages you want to send per month and to whom. Carrier fees
Mobile carriers charge fees to deliver messages to subscribers on their networks. These fees vary by country, carrier, and message type, and can change as carriers update their pricing.

Klaviyo bundles carrier fees into your plan rate. The rate shown for each message type and region is the all-in cost — there are no separate carrier fee line items on your invoice.

Comparing rates across providers
Not all providers include carrier fees in their advertised rates. Some show a base per-message rate and add carrier fees as a separate charge at billing, which means the rate you're quoted may not reflect what you'll actually pay.

When comparing messaging rates across platforms, ask whether the quoted rate includes carrier fees. With Klaviyo, it does.

WhatsApp

Klaviyo follows Meta’s billing model: charging per template instead of per conversation. This means that there is a charge for any delivered marketing or utility/transactional template message.

In Klaviyo, WhatsApp messages are billed at a per-message rate using the same system as your overall Mobile Messaging Plan. After you purchase a Mobile messaging plan, you can it as you see fit for SMS, MMS, WhatsApp, or any combination.

How WhatsApp messages are billed

Every Klaviyo customer gets $5 of Mobile Messaging dollars to spend on Klaviyo monthly for free. WhatsApp usage is billed according to the WhatsApp rate per-message pricing associated with your plan.

Because WhatsApp fees and currency may vary by region and plan type, you can always find your up-to-date WhatsApp pricing by checking your Billing page in Klaviyo or reviewing your Klaviyo order form or contract, which lists the price per WhatsApp message.

If you have questions about your specific WhatsApp rates or currency, contact Klaviyo Support or your account representative and include your account ID and region.

Composer is Klaviyo's AI marketing agent. It analyzes your account, audits your work, recommends next steps, drafts content, and QAs what you've built. Note: Klaviyo may update its Credit calculation methodology from time to time with the then current consumption rates published in the Documentation. Credits are non-transferable, non-refundable, and expire at the end of each billing period with no rollover.

Managing Limits, Billing Preferences, and Overages

What happens when you reach your plan’s limits depends on what type of product it is.

Consumption-based products like Email, Mobile Messaging, Composer, Helpdesk, and Customer Agent operate similarly when overages are incurred.

Meanwhile, products like Advanced KDP, Marketing Analytics, Social Marketing, and Customer Hub are all based on Active Profiles, which grows as your business grows. These 3 products operate similarly with respect to overages.

Email and Mobile Messaging

For marketing products, what happens varies by which limit you reached (i.e., profile or send) as well as your product type:

Profile limit

When you exceed your plan’s active profile limit, Klaviyo does not stop you from sending or adding additional profiles. However, you are notified and will be moved to a higher plan tier (one with enough profiles) as of your next billing cycle. Profile enforcement is different from the Automatically upgrade setting, which is an optional setting that moves you to the next tier when you reach your message limit.

If you don’t want to be upgraded, you can manage your active profiles to stay within your current plan limits.

If you are on the free Profiles & Email plan and are over your profile limit, you won’t be upgraded but you also cannot send emails (or set flow emails live) until you are under your active profile limit.

Email send & Mobile messaging limits

When you reach your plan’s sending limits (i.e., emails, tickets, sends) on a plan that has consumption-based pricing, there are 3 options you can choose from. You can:

Flexible overage plans (i.e., use flexible sending), which provides the next tier’s amount of sends in full. The cost to flex is based on the unit pricing of your base plan (the plan you started the month on).
Upgrade (manually or via auto-upgrade) to a higher-tiered plan. Upgrading is generally more economical than flex sending if your upgrade needs are consistent.
Stop all sending until the next billing cycle.

Want more information about these options? Jump ahead to this billing preferences section.

Helpdesk & Customer Agent

Helpdesk and Customer Agent plans are similar to Email and Mobile Messaging plans, but Helpdesk is based on tickets and Customer Agent is based on conversations.

For Service products your ability to use these products does not stop when your plan limit is reached. When your purchase Customer Agent or Helpdesk, you are committing to a minimum spend on this product with overages for any additional usage.

There is a usage fee for exceeding your plan's limits. Upon purchasing Helpdesk or Customer Agent, you will be automatically enrolled into Flexible Overages as your default billing preference option for any overages that you incur. You may change this billing preference at any time to Auto-Upgrade in Billing > Billing Preferences.

You must be enrolled in either Flexible Overages or Auto-Upgrade in order to continue using Helpdesk and Customer Agent. If you wish to stop accruing overages for these products, you may turn Customer Agent or Helpdesk off within the product or cancel your plan(s).

Reviews

Review plans are similar to email and SMS plans, but are based on orders from your store rather than messages.

If your plan allows you 20 orders, you can send review requests for only those orders. When you reach your plan’s limit, you can either upgrade manually or pause sending review requests until your billing cycle renews.

Advanced Klaviyo Data Platform (KDP)

Advanced KDP plans are priced so that you pay based on the selected plan and for overages beyond that plan.

There is a usage fee for exceeding a plan’s limits. The fee is defined as: the number of profiles that exceed your current tier (in 1,000s) multiplied by the price per 1,000 profiles at the current tier.

Example of usage fee

Say that the company Funky T-shirts pays $4,765 per month for a Advanced KDP plan that allows 1 million profiles. However, the next month, their profile count grows to 1.52 million, exceeding their plan by 520,000.

Instead of forcing Funky T-shirts to upgrade to the next tier (2 million profiles at $9,100), they pay a usage fee that is:

Number of 1,000 profiles that exceed the current tier: 520
X
Price per 1,000 profiles at the current tier: $4.77
=
Total usage fee: $2,480.40.

This means that between their plan ($4,765) and usage fee ($2,480.40), the total cost is $7,245.40. This is less than what Funky T-shirts would pay if they upgraded. Unlike with other products (where you pay before), this fee is billed after usage.

Generally, if you’re using less than 85% of the next tier up, it is more cost-efficient to incur the usage fee. Once you’re using 85% or more of the next tier, it’s better to upgrade.

The fee is applied to the next month’s invoice.

How to choose a different upgrade/downgrade option
Use flexible overages 

Flexible sending is available for Email, Mobile Messaging, Helpdesk, and Customer Agent plans. Flexible sending is not available for Reviews, Advanced KDP, Marketing Analytics, and Customer Hub plans.

When you hit your plan’s limit while using flexible overages, it is a 1-time add-on of all of the profiles and messages included in the next highest tier without permanently upgrading your account. This way, you stay on your current plan and don’t need to worry about downgrading before the next billing cycle.

The amount it costs to flex is based on the unit cost of your current plan and the capacity in the following tier(s). Flexing is a one-time purchase that includes all of the capacity units in the next tier (i.e. # of email sends) at the unit rate (cost per send) of your current plan.

This means that while there are no additional fees to flex, it generally costs more than upgrading, as upgrading makes you eligible for a reduced cost per unit with a higher volume.

Note: Flexible sending for Mobile Messaging works differently. If you are enrolled in Flexible sending for Mobile Messaging, you will incur a flexible overage charge that represents the number of dollars you are purchasing from the next tier rather than message units.

You can check how much it will cost to flex to the next tier before you send, either in the Billing Preferences tab or at checkout. After your purchase has taken place, you will receive an invoice with the charged amount.

What happens when I’m automatically upgraded?

When you upgrade, you pay the difference between your base plan (i.e., the plan you entered the billing cycle with) and the plan you’re upgrading to during that first month.

It is recommended that you upgrade (rather than flexing) if you have flexed more than a few times a year, it can be more cost effective.

Manually upgrade or stop sending: use the None option 

If you want to manually choose when you upgrade or to stop sending when you reach your plan’s limits, you can set your billing upgrade preference to the None option. Go to Settings > Billing > Preferences.

This means that you won’t be able to send any flow and campaign messages, and any messages scheduled during the current billing cycle will be canceled. However, if you decide to upgrade or flex plans, you can resume sending.

Note: The None option is only available for Email and Mobile Messaging (SMS) plans.

Adjust credit card details

Klaviyo does not support multiple credit cards in a single account. If you purchase a plan with a different credit card than the one in your account, this changes the credit card on file. If your payment ever fails, the account Owner and billing contact will be alerted via email.

For details on how to change the credit card on file, check out our article on how to update your credit card information. To see invoices and other details regarding your recent charges, select Billing > Payment History.
