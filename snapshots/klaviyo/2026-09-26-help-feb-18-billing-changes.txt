Why is Klaviyo implementing these pricing changes?

We are introducing pricing changes to simplify billing, improve customer flexibility, and ensure plans align with your value. These changes address customer concerns about confusing or frustrating pricing structures and provide greater control over costs.

What are the key changes effective February 18, 2025?
Profile plan compliance: customers will be required to be on an email plan that matches or exceeds their active profile count.
Flexible sending options: with this billing preference option selected, Klaviyo will automatically upgrade you to the plan you need during high send periods and automatically downgrade you at your next billing cycle.
Auto-downgrade option: with this billing preference option selected, Klaviyo will downgrade your email plan if your active profile count qualifies for a lower tier at the start of each billing cycle.
Who do these changes affect?

These changes will apply to all customers who pay via credit card.

When will the new billing preferences (flexible sending and auto-downgrade) be available?

You will see them on your billing preferences page as options beginning February 18, 2025.

Will my existing billing preferences change?

No. You will need to proactively select the new billing preferences.

What happens if my active profiles exceed my plan limit?

For paid accounts, if your active profiles exceed your plan limit:

Your plan will automatically upgrade to the next tier at the start of your next billing cycle.
Notifications will be sent to account Owners and Admins each time:
You are approaching your plan limit.
An upgrade occurs.

For free accounts, you will not be able to send messages, turn flows live, etc. until your active profile count is below your plan limit.

Example:

You have 64,000 active profiles and are paying for a plan that allows for up to 65,000 active profiles.

You later add 5,000 more active profiles at the start of your next billing cycle, bringing your total number of active profiles up to 69,000. If you keep all of these active profiles, Klaviyo would automatically move you to the next email plan tier that meets your active profiles requirement at the end of your billing cycle (i.e., move you to a plan that includes 70,000 active profiles).

If you delete or suppress 4,000 or more active profiles (i.e., stay under the 65,000 active profile limit) before the end of your billing cycle, you will stay on your current plan.

How can I manage my profiles to stay within budget?

How does flexible sending work?

Flexible sending allows customers to temporarily increase sending and active profile capacity during high-demand periods without upgrading their plan.

How it works:

Turn on flexible sending to receive the benefit whenever you have increases in usage for products like Email, Mobile Messaging, Helpdesk, and Composer
Once you’ve hit your usage limit, you’ll be billed upfront and entitled to the next tier’s capacity at the unit price of the current commit amount.
It resets to your original plan at the start of the next billing cycle.

Example for Email:

You have flexible sending selected for your Email plan and are on a $80/month plan that entitles you to 35,000 monthly emails (the CPM rate of this plan is therefore $1.14 per thousand emails). You also need 10,000 more emails than your plan entitles this month due to increased volume.

After you use your 35,000 emails, Klaviyo will entitle you to the capacity of the next Email plan tier (in this case, 50,000 emails).

For this, you'll be charged $17.10.

Heres the math: 15,000 emails * ($1.14 / 1000) as your unit rate = $17.10.

At the start of the next billing cycle, you will return to the $80 plan that entitles you to 35,000 monthly emails.

Who benefits:

Ideal for customers with occasional surges, like during Black Friday or seasonal campaigns.

Flexible sending is based on the unit price of your current plan (i.e., also called the variable unit cost). Frequent use may make upgrading more cost-effective.

Example for Mobile messaging:

You're on a $900/month plan with Flex turned on. After using your full $900 of plan spend, you send $112.50 worth of additional messages before your billing cycle ends.

Because Flex is on, you're billed that additional $112.50 one-time rather than being upgraded.

At the start of your next cycle, you're back on your $900 plan.

You will receive an invoice that will detail this charge which you can see in Billing > Settings > Payment History. The “Amount” column represents your Flex Overage charge. The Quantity and Unit Price line items do not apply.

Note that flexible sending for SMS is available for plans priced $490/month and higher.
