What is Klaviyo Data + Analytics?
Klaviyo Data + Analytics is a suite of advanced analytical tools designed to help businesses unlock deeper insights from their marketing and customer data. It combines powerful reporting, predictive analytics and segmentation features to give you a complete view of customer behaviour across channels. With Klaviyo Data + Analytics, you can create custom reports, track performance with advanced attribution and forecast customer lifetime value (CLV) to make smarter, data-driven marketing decisions.

How do Klaviyo’s built-in analytics differ from the Personalization add-on?
Klaviyo includes standard reporting on campaign, flow and audience performance in every account. The Personalization add-on unlocks deeper, more customizable insights – such as advanced attribution, custom conversion metrics, multi-touch modelling (coming soon), predictive analytics and RFM segmentation.  With Personalization, you can track full customer journeys, benchmark performance against similar brands and visualise the impact of marketing across channels – all in one place.

What kinds of reports and dashboards are available in Klaviyo Analytics?
"Klaviyo Analytics provides a mix of pre-built dashboards and custom reporting tools to track every part of your marketing funnel. You can access: · Marketing performance dashboards for flows, campaigns and sign-up forms · Custom reports built around your own KPIs · Conversion overview and audience performance reports · Attribution and revenue dashboards for tracking true ROI · Segment, list and growth reports for understanding acquisition and retention trends All reports are customisable with filters, metrics and visualizations – helping you analyse performance and identify actionable insights quickly."

What factors determine the price of Klaviyo’s Data + Analytics plans?
"Pricing for Klaviyo’s Data + Analytics plans depends on your profile volume and the specific analytics products you choose: · Personalization pricing is based on your active profiles (contacts you market to) · Advanced Klaviyo Data Platform (CDP) pricing is based on the total number of profiles stored in your account As your list size or data volume grows, pricing scales accordingly. You can use the pricing slider on Klaviyo’s website to estimate your monthly cost."

Can I combine Personalization and the Advanced Data Platform in one plan?
Yes, you can combine Personalization and the Advanced Klaviyo Data Platform for a unified analytics and data management solution. Together, these add-ons provide end-to-end insight – from marketing attribution and predictive analytics to full data unification and segmentation across all touchpoints. The combined plan’s pricing reflects your active and total profile counts.

Do I need a minimum number of profiles to use Personalization or the Advanced Data Platform?
There’s no minimum requirement to access Klaviyo’s Data + Analytics products. However, pricing tiers are based on your number of profiles – so smaller brands with fewer contacts will pay less, and costs scale as your customer base grows. This makes it flexible for both start-ups and enterprise teams to begin at the right level and expand as they scale.

Can I try Personalization or the Advanced Data Platform before committing?
Yes, Klaviyo offers trial periods for Data + Analytics products so you can explore the features and see how they fit your business needs before making a long-term commitment. Contact your account manager or Klaviyo support to learn more about trial options.
