ABOUT KLAVIYO
The B2C CRM

Klaviyo is the AI-first CRM built for B2C brands. Powered by its built-in data platform and AI insights, Klaviyo combines marketing automation, analytics, and customer service into one unified solution, making it easy for businesses to know their customers and grow faster.

HOW IT STARTED
Built to solve the customer data problem

In 2012, Klaviyo co-founders Andrew Bialecki and Ed Hallen saw a frustrating pattern: consumer brands were sitting on mountains of data but couldn’t use it to personalize experiences or drive revenue.

The tech to unite data, analytics, and marketing didn’t exist. So they built it.

Today, 205,000+ brands worldwide use Klaviyo not just to sell but to create lifelong customer relationships.

“At Klaviyo, we’ve always believed that building customer relationships starts with your data—and it shouldn’t be complicated. We help businesses of all sizes unlock the full potential of their data to build lifelong relationships and grow smarter, faster, and without compromise. That’s the power of customer-first data.”

—Andrew Bialecki, Klaviyo co-founder and CEO

HOW KLAVIYO HAS EVOLVED
A data-first platform from day one. Now a unified B2C CRM.

2012: Klaviyo is founded as a customer database.

2013: Email marketing launches.

2018: Marketing attribution and optimization launches.

2021: SMS marketing launches. 7.5 billion emails sent during BFCM.

2023: Reviews launches.

2023: CDP launches.

2025: B2C CRM launches: unifying data, marketing, service, and analytics.

What’s with the name?

Klaviyo (clay-vee-oh) is inspired by clavija, a Spanish word for mountaineering pins—also called pitons. These pitons support climbers as they ascend, and catch them if they fall.

We liken our customers to mountaineers. Our goal is to support and accelerate their climb to the summit.
