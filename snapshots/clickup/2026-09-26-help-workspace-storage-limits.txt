Workspace storage limits

Workspace storage includes:

Attachments uploaded to tasks, List info, Notepad, or Docs.
Attachments uploaded to Chat.
Cover images uploaded from your device. Cover images can be added to Forms, tasks, Docs, Board view, and Team Overviews.
Voice and screen Clip recordings.
Workspace storage limits per plan

This table shows the Workspace storage available for all ClickUp Plans:

Plan	Workspace storage
Free Forever	60MB
All paid plans	Unlimited
