Intro to ClickUp Brain AI
Which features are included with the Brain AI add-ons?

ClickUp Brain AI is purchased as add-ons to your plan. This article explains which Brain AI features are available in each add-on.

Feature availability and limits vary by plan and user role. Learn more

Some Workspaces see a new set of plans that include more ClickUp AI. Your current plan and features do not change.

Skills extend Brain and Super Agent capabilities. Learn more

AI add-ons

This table details the pricing model and features included in the add-ons:

Add-on	Details
Bundle and save: Everything AI add-on (Formerly AI Autopilot Bundle)	The Everything AI add-on is purchased per Workspace member per month and automatically renews.
Brain AI (Formerly AI Standard)	Get unlimited access to Brain. The Brain AI add-on is purchased per Workspace member per month and automatically renews.
AI Talk to Text	Use your voice to quickly create comments and content across ClickUp.
The AI Talk to Text add-on is purchased per Workspace member per month and automatically renews. It includes unlimited transcription.
AI Notetaker	The AI Notetaker add-on includes 20 free hours. To add more, purchase a monthly block of hours. Blocks start at $12 per month for 60 hours.
AI Super Credits	AI Credits fuel your Workspace's Super Agents. They do not roll over into the next month and automatically renew.
AI Super Credits are currently $0.001 per credit. You can purchase credits monthly in $10 increments.
For example, if you purchase $10 of credits, you'll have a usage limit of 10,000 AI Super Credits that month across the features included in the AI Super Credit add-on.
