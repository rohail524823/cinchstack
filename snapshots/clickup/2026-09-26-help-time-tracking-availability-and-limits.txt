Time Tracking feature availability and limits

Track time spent on software implementations, client deliverables, or any kind of work with ClickUp time tracking.

Timesheet and timesheet approvals features and plan availability are subject to change. You can try out these new features with unlimited usage on any plan for a limited time.

Time tracked via integrations

Use of time tracking integrations count towards time tracking use limits.

Time tracked via ClickUp Brain AI

Time entries created by Brain and Super Agents count toward the same time tracking limits as time entries created manually.

ClickUp Brain AI feature availability and limits vary by plan and user role. Learn more
