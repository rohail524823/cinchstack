Before you start
‣ Confirm client and job data in Jobber is accurate and up-to-date

Installation steps
‣ Log in to your HighLevel account and go to Settings → Integrations
‣ Select Jobber and click Connect
‣ Authorize HighLevel to access your Jobber account via secure OAuth
‣ Choose sync preferences: Clients, Jobs, and Job Updates
