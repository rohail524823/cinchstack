FAQ
How does Jobber create my marketing plan?

Jobber builds your Marketing Plan from the information already in your account, including the services you sell, the customers and industry you serve, and when your busy seasons hit. It plans activities across email, social, Google, and your Jobber website to help you generate work. As your business changes, so does your plan. Every recommendation shows the reasoning behind it, so you're approving a plan you understand rather than a black box.

Do I need marketing experience to use Marketing Tools?

No. Marketing Tools is built for busy service business owners who don't have time to plan, create, and execute their marketing. Jobber works out what to promote and when, drafts the content, and puts it in front of you to approve, edit or reject. You bring what you know about your business, and Jobber will put your marketing to work.

Do I stay in control of what gets published?

Yes. Nothing is published under your business name until you approve it. You can approve a draft as written, change anything you'd say differently, or skip it entirely, and skipping one thing doesn't break the rest of your plan. Over time, Jobber will learn from your feedback and sharpen your plan and content going forward.

What marketing channels are included in Marketing Tools?

Marketing Tools covers your Jobber Website, Google Profile, email campaigns, social posts, customer reviews, referrals, and online booking. They work together rather than separately—one completed job can become a website update, a social post, and a fresh photo on your Google Business Profile all at once, so your business looks current everywhere customers check.

What does Marketing Tools cost?

Your Jobber Website and Google Business Profile optimization are included with every Jobber plan at no additional cost. Marketing Suite is a paid add-on that adds your Marketing Plan, Dashboard, email campaigns, social and Google posting, job showcases, and referrals and reviews.

Learn more about Jobber pricing

How does AI help with my marketing?

AI writes the first draft so you don't have to. It pulls from your jobs, services, and reviews to create a plan and draft emails, social posts, Google posts, and review replies, then hands them to you to approve, edit, or skip. It never publishes anything on its own. Jobber AI can even produce a customized website for free in just a few minutes.

How much time does this take each week?

A few minutes. Marketing Tools is designed to minimize the time required each week — you open your dashboard, see what's queued, and approve or adjust it. The planning, writing, and scheduling happen before you sit down.

How do I know my marketing is working?

Businesses powered by Jobber’s Marketing Tools grow their revenue an average of 2x faster than Jobber customers without it. Instead of guessing whether a campaign helped, you can see trends month over month—like referrals resulting in new work or campaign lead conversion—and decide what's worth doing again.

Jobber’s Marketing Tools features like referrals, campaigns, and reviews all provide actionable information you can use to guide your marketing efforts.
