What is the AI Search Add-on?

The AI Search Add-on extends SE Ranking’s AI functionality and helps you track, analyze, and optimize brand visibility across leading AI platforms.

The Add-on works directly inside your SE Ranking account and does not require additional tools or integrations.

While AI Search features are available as part of SE Ranking subscription plans, the Add-on provides extended limits and advanced capabilities for teams working with multiple brands and larger datasets.

What’s included

AI Results Tracker: extended limits

Additional tracking limits across all supported AI platforms:

AI Overviews
AI Mode
ChatGPT
Perplexity
Gemini

AI Research in Competitive Research: expanded scale

AI Research is available in SE Ranking subscription plans with defined limits. With the AI Search Add-on, users can:

Analyze an unlimited number of brands
Conduct AI Research across five AI engines without brand-based restrictions

This is designed for agencies and teams managing multiple projects.

Access to SE Visible 

The AI Search Add-on includes access to SE Visible , a standalone product focused on strategic AI visibility insights.

Note: AI Research in Competitive Research is available with defined limits in subscription plans and can be expanded with the AI Search Add-on.

Pricing

1 check = 1 prompt + 1 AI platform

Example: tracking one prompt across all five supported AI engines (AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini) uses five checks — one check per prompt, per engine.

FAQ

Q What’s the difference between SE Visible and the AI Search Add-on?

A SE Visible is a standalone product designed for marketing leaders and agencies who need a strategic overview of brand visibility across AI platforms.

The AI Search Add-on expands AI functionality inside SE Ranking and supports operational workflows, including:

AI prompts monitoring
AI sources analysis
AI visibility tracking
AI competitive research

These tools serve different use cases and can be used together.

Q What’s planned for the AI Search Add-on?

A The AI Search Add-on continues to evolve and is expected to include:

More detailed competitive research with gap identification
Additional actionable insights and recommendations
Advanced prompts research capabilities

Q What if the tracking limits included in the Add-on are not enough?

A The AI Search Add-on is available in predefined tracking packages, allowing you to choose a starting limit that fits your needs. If the selected package does not provide sufficient tracking capacity, you can upgrade to a larger package at any time. For advanced requirements, it is also possible to request a custom package with a specific number of checks by contacting the Sales team.

Q How do I get access to SE Visible after purchasing the Add-on?

A After purchasing the AI Search Add-on:

A SE Visible switcher becomes available in your SE Ranking account
Use the switcher to proceed to SE Visible

Complete a separate registration for SE Visible (no additional payment required)
You will receive an email to set up your account password
After logging in, set up your brand manually

Brands are not created automatically because SE Visible uses a separate analysis system. Once setup is complete, you can use SE Visible with full access included in the Add-on.

Q How do AI Search limits and “checks” work?

A Your subscription plan and AI Search Add-on package each include a set number of checks per month. As shown in the Pricing section above, 1 check = 1 prompt tracked on 1 AI platform — so tracking one prompt across three platforms (for example ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity) uses three checks. When your monthly checks are used up, you will see a “limit reached” message and won’t be able to add another prompt or website until the limit renews or you upgrade to a larger package.
