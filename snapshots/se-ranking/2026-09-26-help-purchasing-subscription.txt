Purchasing a subscription from the trial account

To upgrade the free trial to a paid account, log in to your account and go to the Billing page.

Choose the subscription period and product; checks are performed daily. Click “Continue with” to proceed to the Checkout page of our billing partner Stripe.

You have the option to pay either as a Person or as a Company using the business name. To avoid paying VAT, choose to make payments as a Company and add the VAT ID to a separate field.

 

After purchasing a subscription plan, the trial account will be deactivated.

Upgrading a subscription

You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time.

How to change: Go to Account Settings → Billing and click Buy subscription. Select the plan you’d like to switch to and click Buy.

Cost calculation: The system automatically calculates the price difference between your current plan and the new one. You’ll be able to review the final amount on the checkout page.

Subscription dates: If you don’t change the billing period (for example, monthly to monthly), your new plan keeps the same end date. If you change the period (for example, monthly to annual), the system adjusts the dates; your new subscription starts on the day you make the change and the end date is based on the new period.

Credits for downgrades: If you switch to a lower-priced plan, the unused balance will be credited to your Stripe account. This balance will automatically be used for any future SE Ranking purchases.

Renewing a subscription

To renew a subscription plan, go to the Billing page and click the corresponding button.

Please note: If you do not extend the subscription plan within 60 days, the data in the account will be deleted.

The email about the auto-renewal that includes instructions on how to cancel your subscription is sent 7 days before the renewal.

To disable auto-renewal, follow these instructions.

Invoices

All your invoices are available in the Invoices section of your SE Ranking account.
