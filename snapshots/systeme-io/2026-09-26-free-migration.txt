The systeme.io free migration service is a done-for-you business transfer where the systeme.io migration team moves your existing products, sales pages, funnels, email lists, and customer data from your current platform to systeme.io. There is no downtime requirement and no data is deleted from your original platform during the process.

Frequently asked questions about the systeme.io free migration service
How does the free migration to systeme.io work?

The process has three steps. First, sign up for the Unlimited plan or an annual plan. Second, contact systeme.io support and provide access to your current platform (screenshots, export files, or read-only login where available). Third, the systeme.io team rebuilds your funnels, pages, email sequences, products, and contacts in your new account. You receive a time estimate before work begins. You do not need to do any of the technical transfer yourself.

Which platforms can you migrate from?

The systeme.io migration team handles moves from ClickFunnels, Kajabi, Teachable, Kartra, Podia, Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, Keap, WordPress/WooCommerce, and most other major online business platforms. If your platform is not on this list, contact support before signing up to confirm your setup can be migrated.

How long does migration take?

Timeline depends on the size and complexity of your business. A simple migration — one funnel, an email list, and a few products — typically takes 2 to 5 business days. Larger migrations with multiple courses, complex automation sequences, and large contact lists can take 1 to 2 weeks. The team provides a written estimate after reviewing your current setup.

Is the free migration really 100% free? What are the requirements?

Yes, there is no additional fee for the migration service itself. It is included with the Unlimited monthly plan ($97/month) or any annual systeme.io plan. You do not need to cancel your existing platform before migration begins — the team works in your new systeme.io account while your old platform remains live. You only cancel the old tools once you have confirmed everything is working correctly in systeme.io.

Will there be downtime or data loss when I migrate?

No downtime is required on your end. The migration happens in your new systeme.io account, not on your live business. Your current funnels, pages, and emails stay active throughout. Once migration is complete, you switch your domain and links to point to systeme.io, then cancel your old tools. No data is deleted from your original platform during the process.
