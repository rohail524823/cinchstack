What is systeme.io?

Systeme.io is a free all-in-one online business platform that combines email marketing, sales funnels, website builder, online courses, affiliate management, and marketing automation in a single account. It was built by Aurelien Amacker in 2018 with a clear goal: give every entrepreneur access to professional business tools without expensive subscriptions or technical complexity. The platform is free for up to 2,000 contacts with no transaction fees. Paid plans start at $17/month. Over 500,000 entrepreneurs use systeme.io to run their online businesses

Frequently asked questions about choosing systeme.io
Why should I choose systeme.io over using multiple separate tools?

With multiple tools, you pay for each one separately and spend time connecting them via Zapier or API. A typical stack — ClickFunnels ($97/month), Mailchimp ($20/month), Teachable ($39/month), and an affiliate tool ($89/month) — costs around $245/month. systeme.io covers all four for $0 to $97/month, and because everything is in one system, there is no integration to break. One login, one contact database, one support team.

Is systeme.io good for beginners with no technical skills?

Yes — it was specifically designed for non-technical users. The page editor, funnel builder, course platform, and workflow builder all use drag-and-drop. There is no coding, no hosting to configure, and no plugins to maintain. Most beginners can build and launch their first funnel in under an hour using the built-in templates. The free plan means you can test everything before spending a cent.

Can I run my entire online business on systeme.io?

For most digital businesses — yes. systeme.io covers funnels, email, courses, website, blog, affiliate program, booking calendar, CRM, communities, and physical product sales. The team at systeme builds their own business on the platform (they use it themselves). The main limits are that it is not designed for large-scale e-commerce with complex inventory, and it does not offer branded mobile apps for communities.

How does systeme.io compare to all-in-one platforms like Kajabi or ClickFunnels?

systeme.io is cheaper and has a permanent free plan — Kajabi starts at $69/month with no free option, ClickFunnels at $97/month. systeme.io covers a broader feature set (email + funnels + courses + affiliates + CRM + booking + communities) than either of those platforms at the same price point. The trade-off is that Kajabi has more polished course delivery UX and ClickFunnels has a larger community and more advanced funnel split-testing features.

Does systeme.io offer a free plan and what's included?

Yes. The free plan is permanent — no trial period, no credit card required. It includes: 2,000 contacts, unlimited email sends, 3 sales funnels, 1 course, 1 affiliate program, 1 custom domain, and the full suite of tools (website builder, CRM, automation, booking calendar, communities). The paid Startup plan at $17/month removes the funnel and course limits and raises the contact cap to 5,000.
