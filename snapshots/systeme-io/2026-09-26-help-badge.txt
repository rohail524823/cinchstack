How to remove the affiliate badge from your page

This article will show you how to remove the affiliate badge from your page.

What you'll need:

A systeme.io account with a paid subscription plan (Startup, Webinar, or Unlimited)
A sales funnel page

Navigate to your sales funnel and click Edit page (1) to open the editor.

Click Settings (2) at the top left of your screen.

Scroll down the settings menu to the Display an affiliate badge to earn lifetime commissions checkbox (3).

Uncheck the box to remove the affiliate badge from your page.

IMPORTANT: Users on the Free plan cannot remove the affiliate badge.
