How to cancel a subscription to systeme.io or an offer hosted on systeme.io

In this article, you will learn how to cancel your subscription to systeme.io or to an offer that is hosted on our platform.

To access your subscription settings, click your Profile Picture, then Settings (1). From the settings menu on the left, click Manage my subscriptions (2) to display a list of your subscriptions.

On the subscription you want to cancel, click the 3 dots, then Cancel subscription (3).

A popup will be displayed asking to confirm the cancellation. Click Confirm (4) to cancel your subscription.

IMPORTANT:

The cancellation will take effect just before the next expiration date.
The resource is removed automatically, so if a customer cancels their subscription, they will no longer have access to their subscription plan or the offer they originally subscribed to.
Only subscriptions will be displayed in this section; offers that are paid in installments cannot be cancelled using this method.

Note: The dates and times displayed on your subscription pages automatically follow the format and timezone configured in your main account settings.
