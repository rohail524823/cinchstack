Here’s what you get with Semrush’s Content Toolkit.

Key features:

Unlimited standard articles: Generate as many long-form articles as you want, using AI and Semrush data
SEO-boosted articles: Add advanced SERP analysis to increase your chances of ranking
Topic Finder: Discover high-impact content ideas, backed by real search data
SEO Brief Generator: Build detailed briefs, with keywords, content outlines, and competitor insights
Content Optimizer: Analyze and improve your content with SEO, tone, and readability tips
AI writing tools: Compose copy with AI Chat built into your blog editor and instantly rewrite your text
AI image generation: Create custom visuals directly in your workspace
Brand Voice: Create and save up to 50 unique writing tones to keep your content consistent
Google Docs transfers: Send your drafts to Google Docs directly from the tool
WordPress integration: Publish to up to 100 connected sites with one click
