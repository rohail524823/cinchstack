AI Visibility

The Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit shows you how brands appear in AI-generated answers, helping you measure a new layer of visibility beyond traditional search. This toolkit is designed for SEOs and marketers who want to monitor how their site and competitors are positioned across AI systems.

Go to AI Visibility Toolkit

How Can the AI Visibility Toolkit Help Me?

The AI Visibility Toolkit helps you understand and adapt your strategy for the new landscape of AI-driven search. Use it to:

Benchmark your brand's AI visibility and mentions.
Analyze your brand perception and sentiment compared to competitors.
Discover relevant prompts and topics to target for new visibility. 
Track your daily AI visibility for prompts that matter most to your business.
Audit your site for technical issues that could block AI crawlers.
Identify competitive gaps and opportunities in the AI search landscape.
Turn complex AI data into presentation-ready reports.
Who Should Use the AI Visibility Toolkit?

The AI Visibility Toolkit is built with SMBs, agencies, and mid-market companies in mind. It’s designed for teams and professionals who need practical, scalable insights to strengthen their presence in AI-driven search. It’s especially valuable for:

Marketing and SEO teams looking to benchmark clients against competitors, spot visibility gaps, and refine content strategies for AI visibility
SEOs and full-stack marketers who need to monitor AI search trends, resolve visibility blockers, and adapt strategies for sustained performance in AI-generated results
Business and product managers who want to keep a pulse on brand perception and uncover new opportunities in the evolving AI search landscape
What Does the AI Visibility Toolkit Include?

The toolkit gives you access to specialized reports and metrics for analysis, brand management, and monitoring.

Visibility Overview: Get a benchmark of any brand's overall presence in AI search with the AI Visibility Score. You can analyze this data on a Worldwide scale or drill down into specific available regions. See where your brand appears in AI-generated answers, spot competitor activity, and identify opportunities for growth.
Competitor Research: See how AI platforms position your competitors compared to your brand. Use this report to uncover visibility gaps and identify the prompts and topics where others are getting cited but you aren't.
Prompt Research: Discover and prioritize AI search topics to target. Think of it as keyword research for AI—you can see topic-level volume, difficulty, and user intent to find new opportunities.
Brand Performance: Understand how AI platforms perceive your brand against competitors. This suite of reports analyzes your Share of Voice, brand sentiment, and the key narratives driving your reputation to provide AI-generated strategic recommendations.
Prompt Tracking: Monitor your visibility for specific, high-value prompts on platforms like Google AI Mode and ChatGPT. You can track your presence day-by-day to measure the results of your optimizations and act quickly on any sudden drops.
AI Search Site Audit: Check your website for technical issues that might prevent AI bots from crawling your content. The Al Search Health widget in Site Audit flags potential blockers so you can ensure your site is fully accessible.

Billing for Annual Subscriptions

If you add the AI Visibility Toolkit to an existing annual subscription, the purchase will align with your current annual billing cycle. Instead of being charged monthly, you will pay a one-time prorated amount for the remaining time left in your annual subscription term.

For example, if your annual SEO Toolkit (SEO Classic) subscription runs from January 1 to December 31 and you add the AI Visibility Toolkit on July 1, you will be charged a one-time prorated amount covering the remaining six months (July–December). The AI Visibility Toolkit will renew and expire on the same date as your annual subscription.

Access & Sharing in Corporate Accounts 

In a corporate account with multiple users, access to the AI Visibility Toolkit can be shared across your team by adding another license ($99) for each subuser on the account.

This gives your teammate access to AI Analysis reports, Prompt Tracking, and their own separate set of Brand Performance reports. 

Additional domains or locations for Brand Performance can be purchased for $99 each.

Note: Adding an additional domain increases the number of domains you can analyze in Brand Performance—it does not grant another user access.

How to Get More Limits for the AI Visibility Toolkit

There are two ways to increase the limits of your AI Visibility Toolkit.

With Semrush One

Semrush One bundles the SEO and AI Visibility toolkits into three subscription plans (Starter, Pro+, and Advanced), which automatically increase your prompt tracking limits.

Starter: Monitor up to 5 websites in the SEO monitoring tools (e.g., Position Tracking or Site Audit), 50 prompts, and 500 keywords (daily tracking).
Pro+: Monitor up to 15 websites in the SEO monitoring tools, 100 prompts, and 1,500 keywords (daily tracking).
Advanced: Monitor up to 40 websites in the SEO monitoring tools, 200 prompts, and 5,000 keywords (daily tracking).
By Purchasing Add-Ons

You can also purchase additional limits for specific features as needed:

Additional Domain for Brand Performance: You can purchase the ability to track additional domains for $99 per domain per month.
Additional Prompts: You can add 50 more prompts to your Prompt Tracking limit for $60 per month.

How is the AI Visibility Score calculated?

The AI Visibility Score reflects how often your brand is mentioned in AI-generated answers compared to the median number of mentions for your top industry competitors. We automatically identify these competitors to benchmark your visibility within your space.

How do the Brand Performance reports work?

The Brand Performance reports gather a vast collection of common queries and run them through LLMs like ChatGPT and Google's AI Mode. The toolkit then analyzes the AI responses to see how your brand is mentioned, assesses the tone and attributes used, and generates actionable recommendations to refine your brand's positioning.

What are the limits for exporting to CSV?

CSV export in AI Visibility Toolkit reports is available only to users with an active AI Visibility Toolkit or Semrush One subscription. You can export up to 1,000 rows per export, and each user can run up to 10 exports per day.

Is the AI Visibility Toolkit included for all users in a corporate account?

No, the AI Visibility Toolkit is not automatically included for every user in a corporate account. Each user must have their own AI Visibility Toolkit subscription and an assigned access limit in User Management. The toolkit cannot be shared via shared limits, so access must be purchased and assigned separately for each team member.

Can I share AI Visibility Toolkit campaigns or data with others?

Brand Performance reports in the AI Visibility Toolkit are automatically shared with all members of a corporate account who have an AI Visibility Toolkit limit allocated to them. This means that any Brand Performance campaign created within the account is visible to eligible teammates without requiring manual sharing.

However, other AI Visibility Toolkit reports and data are not shared automatically. To distribute insights outside the toolkit, you can export data (such as CSV or PDF files) or create reports using the My Reports tool.
