Local Toolkit Pricing and Plans

Semrush Local tools can be added to your existing Semrush subscription or purchased as a standalone option. Semrush Local offers 2 main plans to suit different business needs: Local Base and Local Pro. There’s also a customizable offer for 20+ location businesses called Local Business — contact our Sales team to learn more.

Note: For businesses with multiple locations, you need a limit for each location. If you're on an older plan, such as Semrush Local Basic, Premium, or Essentials, you don't have access to some of these features. Upgrade your locations in the toolkit to unlock them.

Local Base

Local Base includes GBP AI Agent, which grows your Google Business Profile (GBP) automatically, and Map Rank Tracker, which monitors your Google Maps rankings. The plan costs $30 monthly per location.

Google Business Profile audit and optimization plan: Connect your GBP and get a personalized audit with improvements to increase visibility
Automated post generation and publishing: GBP AI Agent creates, schedules, and publishes unique posts to drive activity and engagement
Auto-replies to reviews: GBP AI Agent replies to new Google reviews as they arrive, using your selected tone of voice and language
Automated photo posting: GBP AI Agent publishes photos you provide at an optimal frequency to keep your profile appealing and trustworthy
Business description optimization: GBP AI Agent creates a keyword-rich description covering what your business is, what it offers, and why customers should choose it
Geo Grid Local Rank Tracking: Track your local rankings on Google Maps using Map Rank Tracker, with 375 credits included
Competitor reports: See where your business stands against others in a detailed heatmap comparison view
Map Rank Tracker reporting API: Build custom reports and simplify multi-location monitoring and analysis
Local Pro

Local Pro provides the GBP AI Agent functionality included in Local Base, plus a broader suite of local marketing tools: Listing Management, Review Management, and GBP Optimization. It also increases your Map Rank Tracker credits. The plan costs $60 monthly per location.

Listing Management
Listing management: Share and manage your business information across 70+ directories with just 1 click. See available directories by country.
Healthcare directories (USA only): Unlock specialized medical directories, such as WebMD, Vitals, and Wellness.com, for US locations with a healthcare-related category
Duplicate and user suggestion management: Track and suppress duplicates, and manage user suggestions
Listing management API: Integrate your listing data with external tools and workflows
Review Management
Centralized review monitoring: Track reviews from supported review sites all in one place
Negative review alerts: Receive email notifications for negative GBP reviews
Hourly review updates: Get GBP review updates in the Local dashboard every hour
Review generation: Run automated SMS or email campaigns to increase the number of online reviews and build credibility with customers
AI-powered GBP review management: Create reply drafts with AI, or set up auto-replies to all GBP reviews or selected categories
Competitor review analytics: Track your competitors’ review progress and see what their customers are saying. Receive up to 10 reports per month, each analyzing up to 10 competitors.
GBP Optimization
GBP data and insights: Explore your business profile views and interactions over time, including historical data unavailable in the GBP interface
GBP posting with AI: Create GBP posts with an AI writer and schedule them weeks or months in advance
GBP edits and suggestions: Monitor edits and suggestions made by others, and accept or reject them
Bulk GBP connect and edit: Connect and manage multiple GBPs at once
Team collaboration: Work with your team on local SEO across shared locations
Location grouping: Group locations by criteria for simpler management and reporting
Map Rank Tracker

GEO grid tracking with Map Rank Tracker with 1,225 credits included.
