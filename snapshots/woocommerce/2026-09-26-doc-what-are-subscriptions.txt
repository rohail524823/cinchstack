What are WooCommerce.com subscriptions?

When you purchase an extension or theme from the WooCommerce.com Marketplace, you receive a WooCommerce.com subscription. This subscription grants you access to product updates, support, and downloadable files for the duration of your subscription term.

You can view and manage all of your subscriptions from the My Subscriptions section of your WooCommerce.com account dashboard.

Note: WooCommerce.com subscriptions are different from the WooCommerce Subscriptions extension, which allows store owners to sell subscription-based products to their customers. This page covers subscriptions for products purchased from the WooCommerce.com Marketplace.

What does a subscription include?

Each WooCommerce.com subscription gives you:

Product updates and improvements — Receive the latest versions of your extension or theme, including compatibility updates for newer versions of WordPress and WooCommerce, bug fixes, and new features.
Customer support — Access support from the product developer or the WooCommerce Happiness Engineers via the Help Desk.
Downloadable product files — Download the latest version of the extension or theme at any time during your active subscription.
30-day money-back guarantee — All WooCommerce.com Marketplace purchases are covered by a 30-day refund policy.
Subscription billing periods

Most WooCommerce.com extension and theme subscriptions are billed on one of the following schedules:

BILLING PERIOD	DESCRIPTION
Annual (1 year)	The most common billing option. Renews every 12 months.
Biennial (2 years)	Available on select extensions, often at a lower per-year cost. Renews every 24 months.

You can see the available billing options on each extension’s product page in the WooCommerce.com Marketplace.

How to change your billing period

To switch from a 1-year to a 2-year subscription (where available), disable auto-renewal on your current 1-year subscription. At the end of your current term, purchase a new 2-year subscription for the product.

How much does renewal cost?

Subscriptions renew at 100% of the current listed price for your selected subscription term. The renewal price is based on the price shown on the product’s WooCommerce.com Marketplace page at the time of renewal — not the price you originally paid.

Coupon codes and promotional discounts do not apply to renewal charges.

What do you receive when your subscription renews?

Renewing your subscription entitles you to continued access to:

Product updates and improvements
Customer support
Compatibility with newer versions of WordPress and WooCommerce
Bug fixes, new features, and enhancements
How many sites can a subscription be used on?

One active subscription key can be used on one active (production) site and one staging, test, or development site at the same time. This allows you to test extensions in both a live and development environment without needing to swap or reactivate keys.

What counts as a separate site:

Subdomains are separate sites — a key activated on example.com does not cover staging.example.com.
Sub-directory sites are separate sites — a key activated on example.com/site1 does not cover example.com/site2.
Protocols — Due to how activation URLs are handled, http://example.com and https://example.com may be counted as separate sites.

Legacy subscriptions: Before February 2020, 5-site and 25-site subscription options were available. If you hold a legacy subscription, it covers that number of active sites plus the same number of staging/test/development sites.

Free product subscriptions

If you have a free extension from the WooCommerce.com Marketplace, your subscription renews automatically at no cost. Free product subscriptions do not require a stored payment method. Auto-renewal keeps your key active so you continue to receive support and updates.

What happens when a subscription expires?

If your subscription expires or is canceled:

You are no longer eligible for product updates or customer support.
The extension or theme remains installed on your site but will no longer receive updates.
Running outdated extensions may lead to compatibility issues with newer versions of WordPress or WooCommerce.

To regain access to updates and support, renew your subscription from the My Subscriptions page or purchase a new subscription.
