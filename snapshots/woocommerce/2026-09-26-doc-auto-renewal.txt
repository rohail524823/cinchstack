Auto-renewal, cancellation, refunds

WooCommerce.com subscriptions renew automatically by default, but you can turn off auto-renew, manually renew, or cancel a subscription at any time. This page explains how each option works, what happens when a subscription expires, and how to reactivate an expired subscription.

Auto-renewal

By default, WooCommerce.com subscriptions are set to auto-renew. When auto-renew is enabled:

Your payment method on file is charged automatically on the renewal date.
The subscription continues without interruption.
You continue to receive updates and support for the extension.

WooCommerce.com sends a reminder email before the renewal date so you know a charge is coming.

Turn off auto-renewal

If you don’t want a subscription to renew automatically:

Log in to your WooCommerce.com account and go to My Subscriptions.
Find the subscription and click the three-dot menu icon (⋮) on the right.
Select Cancel subscription.

When auto-renew is turned off:

You are not charged on the renewal date.
The subscription remains active until its expiration date.
After the expiration date, the subscription expires and the extension no longer receives updates or support.

You can turn auto-renew back on at any time before the subscription expires by returning to My Subscriptions and selecting the option to re-enable it.

Note: Canceling a subscription does not automatically uninstall the extension from your site. The extension remains installed but will no longer receive updates or be covered by support.

Refunds

WooCommerce.com offers a 30-day money-back guarantee on most purchases. For further details about this please see our Refund policy.

What happens if my renewal payment fails?

If your renewal payment fails, we don’t give up right away. Our system automatically retries your payment several times over approximately 26 days, giving you time to update your payment method.

During this period, your subscription is placed on hold. You won’t lose access to your downloads immediately, but you won’t receive product updates or support until the payment is successfully processed.

You’ll receive a few emails during this time letting you know your payment didn’t go through. Each email includes a link to log in and update your payment method. If you update your card, the next scheduled retry will use the new card.

If all retry attempts are unsuccessful, the renewal order is marked as failed and your subscription expires. At that point, you can still renew manually from your My Subscriptions page.

To avoid failed renewals:

Make sure your card on file hasn’t expired. You can check and update it on the Payment Methods page.
Contact your bank if you see repeated failures, as they may be blocking the charge.
What happens when a subscription expires

When a subscription expires (either because auto-renew was turned off or the payment method failed):

The extension stays installed on your site. It is not automatically removed.
Updates stop. You no longer receive new versions of the extension, including security patches and compatibility updates.
Support ends. You can no longer open support tickets for that extension.
Your site may show a notice in the WordPress dashboard indicating that the subscription has expired and updates are unavailable.

Running an outdated extension can lead to security vulnerabilities and compatibility issues with newer versions of WordPress or WooCommerce. It is recommended to either renew the subscription or replace the extension if you choose not to renew.

Renew an expired subscription

You can manually renew an expired subscription from your WooCommerce.com account:

Go to My Subscriptions.
Find the expired subscription — it displays an Expired status.
Click the Renew* option (or the three-dot menu icon ⋮ and select *Renew).
Complete the checkout process to reactivate the subscription.

Once renewed:

Updates and support resume immediately.
The subscription appears as active in your My Subscriptions list.
Once the subscription key is connected, your site can receive extension updates again.

Note: If a significant amount of time has passed since the subscription expired, the renewal price may reflect the current pricing for the extension, which could differ from what you originally paid.

Reactivate an expired or cancelled subscription

In most cases, reactivating an expired or cancelled subscription follows the same process as renewing:

Go to My Subscriptions.
Locate the expired or cancelled subscription.
Use the Renew option to purchase a new subscription term.

If the subscription or product is no longer available on WooCommerce.com, or if you encounter issues reactivating, contact support for assistance.

Failed payments

If your payment method on file fails when your subscription renews:

WooCommerce.com may retry the payment automatically several times over approximately 26 days.
Your subscription is placed on hold during this period.
You receive an email notification about the failed payment.
If the payment continues to fail, the subscription eventually expires.

To resolve a failed payment:

Go to your WooCommerce.com account and update your payment method.
Return to My Subscriptions and manually renew the subscription if it has already expired.

Keeping your payment method up to date helps avoid interruptions in updates and support.
