Change your billing cycle

Learn how to switch between monthly and annual billing for your subscription.

You can change the billing cycle for your Site plan to better match your budgeting and subscription preferences.

Switch from monthly to annual billing

You can upgrade from monthly billing to annual billing at any time.

To switch to annual billing:

Open the project you want to update.

Go to Settings.

Open the Plans tab.

Enable the Yearly billing option.

Click Upgrade below your current subscription.

Review the pricing summary.

Confirm the change.

Your subscription will immediately switch to annual billing.

Switch from annual to monthly billing

Annual-to-monthly billing changes are not currently available through the Framer interface.

To request a change:

Contact the support team through the Contact page.

Include the project link associated with your subscription.

The support team will help process the billing cycle change.

Before switching from annual to monthly billing

Keep the following in mind:

Any unused value from the annual subscription will be converted into account credit.

Account credits are non-refundable and are applied to future invoices.

Monthly billing typically costs more than the equivalent monthly cost of an annual subscription.

FAQ
Can I switch from monthly to annual billing myself?

Yes. You can switch to annual billing at any time from the Plans tab in your project settings.

Can I switch from annual to monthly billing myself?

No. Annual-to-monthly billing changes currently require assistance from the support team.

What happens to unused time on my annual subscription?

The unused value is converted into account credit and automatically applied to future invoices.
