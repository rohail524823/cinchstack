Best use cases for each Framer plan

Learn how to choose the right Framer plan for your site, content, traffic, and team.

Free plan

The Free plan is perfect for hobbyists and personal projects. It includes basic tools to create visually appealing sites on a Free Framer domain, with a “Made in Framer” banner displayed when published. This plan is ideal for exploring Framer’s capabilities without financial commitment. See Why choose Framer for website building?. 

Basic plan

The Basic plan is great for individuals or small teams. You can add up to 20 Locales (add-on), 2 CMS collections, and 30 pages. Which makes this a great option for creating personal sites.

Pro plan

The Pro plan is designed for teams that have a growing business, like startups and scaleups. It comes with features like staging, hosting .well-known files and more. It can grow with you and comes with the option to run A/B tests (add-on) or use the site with a reverse proxy setup.

Enterprise plan

The Enterprise Plan is tailored for large organizations with unique needs. It includes custom hosting, enterprise-grade security, dedicated support, and unlimited capabilities. Visit the Enterprise page to learn more and apply if this plan suits your needs.

For more details about our pricing plans, please visit our pricing page or contact our support team through our contact page.

FAQ
Can I start with the Free plan and upgrade later?

Yes. You can start with the Free plan and upgrade when your site needs more features, higher limits, or a custom domain.

Which plan should I choose for a portfolio or personal site?

The Basic plan is usually the best fit for portfolios and personal sites that need a custom domain. The Free plan is better for testing ideas or learning Framer before publishing.

Where can I find the latest plan limits and pricing?

The Pro plan is a perfect fit for larger businesses, like startups and scaleups. It can grow with you and comes with the option to run A/B tests (add-on) or use the site with a reverse proxy setup. The Enterprise Plan is tailored for large organizations with unique needs, including custom hosting, enterprise-grade security, dedicated support, and unlimited capabilities.
