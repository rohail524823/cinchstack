Site plans overview

Learn how Framer Site plans work and how to choose the right one for your project.

Framer’s flexible pricing lets you pay only for the features you need. Choose from Basic and Pro Site plans, and upgrade as your project requirements or team size grow. See Why choose Framer for website building?.

Site plans

Site plans include the essential tools and features you need to build and publish a professional website. All paid plans come with reliable hosting, so you can focus on designing and shipping your site instead of managing infrastructure.

With a paid Site plan, you can:

Connect a custom domain

Publish your site without Framer branding

Access plan-specific features such as CMS capabilities

When choosing a plan, consider:

The number of pages your site requires

Whether you need CMS functionality

The size and complexity of your project

You don’t need to manually track bandwidth or form submissions. If your site approaches or exceeds your plan’s limits, we’ll notify you so you can upgrade only when necessary.

Editor limits and price per editor

The number of editors you can collaborate with in a Workspace, as well as the price per editor, depends on the highest-tier Site plan in that Workspace.

If your Workspace includes multiple sites, the highest-tier Site plan determines the editor limits and per-editor pricing for the entire Workspace.

Enterprise plan

For larger organizations with advanced requirements, Framer offers custom Enterprise plans.

Enterprise plans are designed for teams that need tailored solutions, additional support, or specific compliance requirements. To explore this option, visit the Enterprise page and fill out the contact form. A sales representative will reach out to discuss your needs.

FAQ
What features are included in Framer’s Site plans?

Site plans provide essential tools and features to create and launch stunning websites. All paid plans include reliable hosting, letting you focus on building instead of managing infrastructure. With a paid plan, you can connect a custom domain, adding professionalism and making it easier for visitors to find your site.

How do I choose the right Site plan for my needs?

Choose a Site plan based on your needs, including the number of pages and additional features like CMS capabilities. You’ll receive notifications if your site exceeds your plan’s limits, so you can upgrade only when necessary.

How are editor limits and pricing determined in Framer Workspaces?

Your editor costs and the number of editors you can collaborate with in your Workspace depend on the highest-tier Site plan in your Workspace.
