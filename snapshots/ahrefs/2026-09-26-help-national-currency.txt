I used to pay for Ahrefs in my national currency. When I try to re-purchase, it shows me a different price. Why?
I used to pay for Ahrefs in my national currency. When I try to re-purchase, it shows me a different price. Why?

Read on to find out why price for subscription in your national currency may differ from what it used to be

Written by Anna
Updated over 3 weeks ago

First off, please note that we have fixed prices only in USD as shown on our Pricing page.

 

If you choose to pay in your national currency, the price will be displayed in that currency using an exchange rate set by Ahrefs. These rates are updated from time to time, so the local currency amount may not exactly match the live exchange rates.

 

If you pay in your national currency (either for a new subscription or an ongoing one), your price might be recalculated using the most recent exchange rate we apply, which may differ from your previous price.

 

If you prefer, you can request payment in USD to avoid exchange rate differences — just contact our support team for assistance.
