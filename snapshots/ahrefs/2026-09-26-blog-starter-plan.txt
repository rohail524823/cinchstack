Ahrefs’ $29 Starter plan – what’s included and how it compares

By Rebekah Bek

Product Marketing at Ahrefs

September 2, 2024
2 min read
5.1K Organic traffic

Article performance data from Ahrefs

If you haven’t heard yet, we’ve launched a new plan at Ahrefs.
Meet our Starter plan: a $29/month offering that’s great for SEO beginners who are looking to go beyond the basics offered in our Ahrefs Free plan.Here’s what you need to know.

Who's the Starter plan for?

The Starter plan is perfect if you're just starting out, or want to explore our platform without a big commitment. It's designed for light usage and gives you a fantastic introduction to the potential of the Ahrefs toolset.

If you end up loving Ahrefs and start using our tools intensively, you may outgrow the Starter plan quickly – but don't worry, upgrading is super easy. You can find a new plan that fits your needs right on our pricing page.

What’s offered in the Starter plan?

Like our Lite plan, the Starter plan is credit-based. On a Starter plan, you’ll get 200 credits each month. You consume these credits when accessing reports and requesting new data from most core tools.You’ll have access to these Ahrefs tools:

SEO Dashboard
Site Explorer
Keywords Explorer (not included in Ahrefs Free)
Site Audit
Rank Tracker (not included in Ahrefs Free)
Ahrefs SEO Toolbar
Web Analytics
Social Media Manager

NOTE

Site Audit and Rank Tracker do not consume credits since they have their own limits.
