What's the difference between all Ahrefs subscription plans?
What's the difference between all Ahrefs subscription plans?

Understand the different limits and features across all subscription plans. Use this guide to better decide which plan best fits your needs.

Written by Constance Tan
Updated this week

Note: Current Standard plans and higher have unlimited fair usage for every active user. If you have a Standard or higher plan that has limited credits per month, you have a legacy subscription. To upgrade to a plan with unlimited credits, go to https://app.ahrefs.com/pricing and select a new plan.

This article gives a more comparative overview of the differences between our subscription plans that can be found on our pricing page.

 

What's included in the free plan

These are

SEO Dashboard

Site Explorer

Site Audit

Web analytics

Social Media Manager

Bot analytics

A full breakdown of all the things you can do free on Ahrefs can be found in this article. 

 

Free versus Starter

Compared to Ahrefs free account, the Starter plan allows you to use:

200 credits to check any website in 

Site Explorer (unverified websites)

Keywords Explorer

Rank Tracker

SERP Comparison

Position History graph

Ahrefs SEO Toolbar (see SEO metrics)

Web Analytics

Bot Analytics (while in beta)

Report Builder

Social Media Manager (unlimited while in beta)

Ahrefs Starter versus Lite

Compared to the Starter plan, the Lite plan allows you to use:

Up to 1000 credits per month on unverified websites

Site Explorer

Keywords Explorer

Competitive Analysis

Page Inspect

Purchase additional credits per month (can be spent by any power user)

Add additional users to your workspace

Export reports

Schedule Alerts

Custom prompts you can use to track AI responses with

Report Builder

Direct access to API v3, Ahrefs MCP, and Ahrefs Connect
