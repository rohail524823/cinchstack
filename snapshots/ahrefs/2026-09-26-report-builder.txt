Questions? We have answers

Can I send reports to people who don’t have an Ahrefs account?

Yes. PDF reports are exported and delivered by email, so recipients don’t need an Ahrefs login.

Does the Text widget count against my widget limit?

No. Text widgets are purely for formatting and don’t fetch metrics, so they don’t count toward your limit.
