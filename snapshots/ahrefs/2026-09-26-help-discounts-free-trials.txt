Do you offer any discounts or free trials?
Do you offer any discounts or free trials?

Everything you need to know about specials on our prices, and other frequently asked questions about discounts, free trials, and more.

Written by Anna
Updated over 3 weeks ago
Do you offer discounts of special offers?

Ahrefs does not run one-off discounts or seasonal specials (this includes Thanksgiving, Black Friday, Cyber Monday etc.) 

 

What we do offer is almost 20% off all annual billing. Check our pricing page for details. 

 

 

To start saving and be billed annually instead of monthly, please contact Support via support@ahrefs.com or our Live Chat. 

 

Do you have anything cheaper than Lite plan?

You can consider purchasing the Starter plan, which is a cheaper monthly plan than Lite. You can also purchase just AI visibility monitoring with our basic custom prompt package, it is also cheaper than Lite.

 

Do you offer discounts for non-profits?

Currently we do not offer any special pricing, discounts or free access for non-profits. ​

​

I found this other website that's reselling Ahrefs at a discounted price. Is it legit?

Purchasing a subscription from an unauthorized third party violates our terms and conditions which can be temporary or permanently blocked with no refund. To be sure that you're purchasing an authentic subscription, please only do it from our pricing page.

 

I have a promotional / discount code, where do I type it to get my access to Ahrefs? 

We do not offer any promotional or discount codes. Such codes that you find on other websites are not legitimate, and should not be trusted.

 

Do you offer any free trials?

We don't provide any free trials, but you can check out our free account, which allows you to check ranking keywords, backlinks and run site audits for websites you can verify ownership for.
