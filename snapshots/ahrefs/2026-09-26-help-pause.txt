Can I pause or suspend my subscription?

Find out if you can pause your Ahrefs subscription indefinitely

Written by Anna
Updated over 3 weeks ago

Unfortunately, there is no option to pause your subscription at Ahrefs. 

 

Instead, you can cancel your subscription and re-subscribe when you're ready to join us again (be sure to click both fields shown on below screenshot to enable "Cancel subscription" button): 
​

 

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period – after which, your account will be downgraded to a Free Ahrefs account.
​
If you have any questions or issues with cancellation, please contact us at support@ahrefs.com or drop us a note in a live chat.
