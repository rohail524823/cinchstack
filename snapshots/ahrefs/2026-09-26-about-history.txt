Ahrefs is a SaaS marketing platform. We help marketers make their brands discoverable—and unmissable—online.

We’re a lean, multinational startup, building products that are meaningful, easy to use, and fast to ship – because in this game, agility really matters. Our motto says it best: First do it, then do it right, then do it better.

Proudly bootstrapped, we’re a diverse bunch from around the world, united by a shared love of technology and a drive to solve real problems. We started in SEO – and we made it pretty far there, if we do say so ourselves 😉. We’ve been in the business of helping people see and be seen on the web for a long time, and that mission hasn’t changed, even as the world around us has.

AI is reshaping how people look for, trust, and choose brands online – fast.

Luckily, we’ve been evolving right along with it.

Marketing doesn’t stand still, and neither do we. With Ahrefs, you can plan, publish, get discovered, measure, and keep improving—all in one place, and built to evolve with wherever search goes next.

WHAT’S NEXT
As the web keeps changing, so does the data behind it. That’s why we’re already thinking about what comes next.

Marketing won’t stand still. Audiences won’t either. Staying visible and making the right moves at the right time will matter more than ever, especially as AI keeps reshaping how people search and decide.

Powering your marketing with data & AI

We’ll keep investing in the infrastructure, AI, and engineering—so you can cut through the noise, navigate a messy, ever-shifting web, and uncover insights that truly matter.

Taking you from data to clear direction

We’ll keep crawling the web, indexing what matters, and expanding what you can track in Ahrefs—so you know what’s working, what’s not, and where your next opportunities lie.

Future-proofing your brand’s online presence
