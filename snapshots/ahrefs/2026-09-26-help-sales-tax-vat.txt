How to get your tax refunded
For United States, Canada, Switzerland, India, Australia, Thailand, Vietnam, Mexico, UK, European Union countries

To get your tax refunded, you'll need to contact our human support team directly. Please email support@ahrefs.com or use live chat and ask to speak with a support agent. Provide your valid tax ID and, if possible, any relevant tax certificates for your organization. Depending on your country, we may ask for additional supporting documents. Once submitted, a support agent will apply the tax details to your account and process a refund for eligible orders made in the last 90 days.

 

For Malaysia, Indonesia, South Africa, Japan and Turkey

Tax is charged on all transactions, so there will be no tax refund option.

 

For orders older than 90 days

Tax amounts have already been passed along to the tax office. You can, however, take your invoice to your local tax office and claim credit or refunds for those, subject to your eligibility to claim input tax credits. It is best to clarify with your local tax representative.
