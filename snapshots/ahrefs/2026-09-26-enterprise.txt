ENTERPRISE FEATURES
Larger teams have different needs. These unique features will make your day-to-day SEO tasks easier.
Portfolios
Group a list of URLs from 10 unique domains and track them as a single project to see their aggregated metrics.
Opportunities
Focus on SEO opportunities to enhance your rankings. Click on any suggestion to be taken directly to the relevant report with all filters pre-applied.
Instant keyword clustering
Instantly cluster keyword ideas by parent topic or by terms. Focus on overarching topics which have all relevant keywords grouped under them.
Page inspect tool
See the full history of changes on a page, including both text content and HTML. Analyze your competitors' on-page tactics and investigate your lost links.
Best links filter
Use the Best links filter and customize it to filter good backlinks from noise.
Raw HTML, rendered HTML, and extracted text
Site Audit shows you all three formats of a page and makes them fully searchable and comparable.
Hreflang link graph
Visualize hreflang issues in a link graph. Quickly spot which links are missing or were added by mistake.
GSC integration
View your website’s GSC performance for more than 16 months. See the distribution of clicks, impressions, and CTR by position right in Rank Tracker.
GSC keywords
See your GSC keywords right within Ahrefs and spot keyword cannibalization issues early on.
GSC pages
See your GSC pages right within Ahrefs and find low-hanging fruit keywords easily.

AI Content Grader
