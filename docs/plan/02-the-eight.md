# 02 — The launch eight

## How they were chosen

Each tool had to pass all five:

1. **Someone running an online business actually buys it.** No developer tools, no enterprise software.
2. **Its pricing is confusing enough that explaining it is a service.** Add-ons, usage fees, seat minimums, tier jumps, "contact sales" walls. If the vendor's pricing page is clear, there is nothing for us to add.
3. **A real affiliate program exists that pays Pakistan** — Impact, PartnerStack (AirWallex), or a direct program with Payoneer/Tipalti payout.
4. **It pays recurring, or a bounty above $100, or on the free trial.** The sister site's lesson: DataCamp paid $0 on 426 clicks because it paid only on a paid conversion.
5. **It sits in one of five layers**, so the eight form a stack rather than a list.

## The five layers

| Layer | Question it answers | Launch tools |
|---|---|---|
| **Sell** | How do I take money? | Shopify, Systeme.io |
| **Site** | Where does my business live online? | Webflow |
| **Grow** | How do people find me and come back? | Semrush, Klaviyo |
| **Run** | How do I manage leads, clients, automation? | GoHighLevel, HubSpot |
| **Organize** | Where does the team's knowledge live? | Notion |

## The eight, one by one

Pricing-confusion is scored 1–5: how far the sticker price is from the real monthly bill at a typical small-business size.

### 1. GoHighLevel — Run — **approved**
- **Program:** direct, Tipalti payout. 40% recurring for the customer's life on Starter ($97), Unlimited ($297), Agency Pro ($497) and White Label Mobile App. 5% second-tier. $50 minimum; **commissions under $50 in a 120-day window are forfeited**. 45-day good-standing hold. Joined 19 Aug 2026. Payout method set up.
- **Pricing confusion: 5/5.** The plan price hides phone/SMS usage (LC Phone), email sending (LC Email), AI add-ons, WhatsApp, premium workflow actions, and rebilling markups. "What does GHL really cost per month" has no honest answer online — the affiliates pushing it don't want one.
- **Rules that become gates:** mandatory disclosure text on every GHL page; no earnings claims; no unauthorized discounts, coupons or trials; no brand terms in domains. Cookie: plan on 30 days.
- **Comparison pairs:** vs HubSpot (the flagship page), vs ClickFunnels (the highest-volume query), vs Systeme.io (the budget question).
- **Verticals:** agencies, dentists, real estate, gyms, coaches, home services — the "GHL for X" pages mirror the sister site's 38 role pages, its strongest ranking pattern.

### 2. HubSpot — Run — apply phase 2
- **Program:** 30% recurring. Network to confirm at application (HubSpot has run on Impact).
- **Pricing confusion: 5/5.** Hubs × tiers × seats × contact bands × onboarding fees. The most-searched "X pricing explained" in B2B SaaS.
- **Pairs:** vs GoHighLevel, vs Klaviyo (email for e-commerce), vs Systeme.io.
- **Why it's in even before approval:** it is the tool GHL is compared against. The page must exist for the GHL cluster to be complete; HubSpot links are plain until approval.

### 3. Shopify — Sell — apply phase 2 (Impact)
- **Program:** up to $150 per merchant who becomes paying, 400-day tracking, Impact. Varies by merchant country.
- **Pricing confusion: 4/5.** Plan price + per-transaction fee + payment processing rate + app subscriptions + theme. Merchants routinely underestimate by 2–3×.
- **Pairs:** vs WooCommerce (huge query; WooCommerce is free — plain link, honest answer), vs Webflow (for stores), vs Squarespace.
- **Stacks:** the Shopify store stack (Shopify + Klaviyo + Semrush + Notion) is one of the six.

### 4. Systeme.io — Sell — apply phase 2 (direct)
- **Program:** 60% lifetime commission, direct. Payout rail to confirm (verify Payoneer/wire).
- **Pricing confusion: 2/5** — its pricing is actually clear, which is its pitch. It earns its place as **the honest budget answer** in every GHL/HubSpot/Kajabi comparison, and as the coach/creator stack's core.
- **Pairs:** vs GoHighLevel, vs Kajabi, vs ClickFunnels.

### 5. Webflow — Site — apply phase 2 (PartnerStack)
- **Program:** 50% of first-year revenue, PartnerStack (pays Pakistan via AirWallex).
- **Pricing confusion: 5/5.** Site plans vs workspace plans vs seats vs CMS items vs bandwidth. Genuinely misunderstood by most buyers.
- **Pairs:** vs Squarespace (very high volume), vs Framer, vs Shopify (for stores), vs WordPress.

### 6. Semrush — Grow — apply phase 2 (Impact)
- **Program:** $200 per new subscription + **$10 per free-trial signup**, 120-day cookie, Impact. The trial payout is the best single term in the set.
- **Pricing confusion: 4/5.** Three tiers plus a growing list of add-ons (Local, Trends, Social, .Trends, AI toolkit) that most buyers need one of.
- **Pairs:** vs Ahrefs (the definitive query; Ahrefs has no affiliate program — plain link, honest answer), vs Moz, vs SE Ranking.

### 7. Klaviyo — Grow — apply phase 2 (verify)
- **Program:** to verify. Klaviyo runs a partner program; whether it has an open affiliate track that pays Pakistan is the open question. **If not, Klaviyo stays on the site with plain links** — its pricing content is valuable regardless — and **Kit (ConvertKit)** becomes the paying email tool, added as tier two.
- **Pricing confusion: 5/5.** Contact-tier pricing that jumps in steps, email vs SMS credits, and a "your bill went up because your list grew" surprise that generates constant search demand.
- **Pairs:** vs Mailchimp (very high volume; Mailchimp has no program — plain), vs Kit, vs HubSpot.

### 8. Notion — Organize — apply phase 2 (PartnerStack)
- **Program:** 50% for 12 months, PartnerStack.
- **Pricing confusion: 3/5.** Per-seat plus AI add-on plus the Plus/Business/Enterprise split; team pricing surprises people.
- **Pairs:** vs ClickUp, vs Coda, vs Obsidian (free — plain), vs Confluence.
- **Why it's in:** highest raw search demand of the eight, and every stack has an Organize layer.

## Tier two — added as demand and approvals justify

Same structure, one at a time, each earning its place by a ranking pricing page first:

- **Run:** ClickFunnels, Kajabi, Keap, monday.com, ClickUp
- **Sell:** Kit, Podia, Teachable, Stan Store
- **Site:** Squarespace, Framer, Wix, Hostinger, Kinsta
- **Grow:** Kit, GetResponse, Brevo, Surfer SEO
- **Organize:** ClickUp, Coda, Airtable

Rule for adding a tool: it must be the honest answer in at least one existing comparison or stack, *and* have a program that pays Pakistan, *or* be so heavily searched that its pricing page ranks on its own (Mailchimp, Squarespace).

## What is deliberately excluded

- **Anything sold to people who want to build AI apps** (boilerplates, prompt packs) — the category collapsed 80–97% in 2026.
- **AI writing tools as a category** — the most saturated affiliate space of 2025–26; only where one intersects a layer (e.g. AI inside Klaviyo).
- **ActiveCampaign** — declined the sister site on PartnerStack. Not on the site until it approves.
- **Coursera / DataCamp / Udemy** — the other site's territory.
